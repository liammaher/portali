What architetto Chiara Fittizia knows about this job, as she'd have it on her desk. Everyone
gets exactly this: the person writing the answer key, the person timing the manual baseline,
and the run (the vault holds the people and firms, `fixture-p6.json`; everything below that
isn't in the vault is what pre-flight has to ask). All of it is made up.

- **Firms:** Edil Fittizia di Marco Esempio (masonry, partitions, finishes) and Impianti

## The property

- Via Domenico Fontana 40, scala B, piano 3, interno 7, 80128 Napoli (Arenella,
  Municipalità 5). Not in the centro storico (zona A), not a listed building, no landscape
  constraint.
- Catasto fabbricati: foglio 12, particella 345, subalterno 7, sezione urbana AVV

- The works touch no common parts of the building and nothing outside the flat.

## The works

- Demolition and rebuilding of two internal partitions (a new layout of kitchen and
  bathroom), new bathroom, new kitchen and bathroom plumbing, electrical system redone.
  Nothing structural; no change of destinazione d'uso; no change of volume or surface.
- Manutenzione straordinaria (art. 3, c. 1, lett. b DPR 380/2001), free of charge: no
  increase in urban load.
- **Already started:** the works began on 19 October 2026 without a CILA, and are still in
  progress. The CILA is filed now, during the works (d.2), with the reduced penalty for a
  late CILA (art. 6-bis, c. 5 DPR 380/2001).
- **Two firms on site**, so the site falls under D.Lgs. 81/2008 and needs the notifica
  preliminare (art. 99); a copy, already sent, is attached (FAC-SIMILE).
- No tolerances to declare; no other communications or assents to request through the SUE.
- The site is small: well under 200 uomini-giorno.
- Luca has no debts to the Comune di Napoli and no collection procedure against him (what he
  declares in the Allegato A). He has no PEC and no landline.
- The penalty for the late CILA is paid before filing and its receipt attached, like the
  151 € diritti.
