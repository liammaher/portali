# Recurring tax obligations of a Naples micro-business (pizzeria, ditta individuale, 3 employees) and where "due" / "done" is recorded

Persona: Gennaro, pizzeria in Naples, ditta individuale, 3 employees, contabilità ordinaria/semplificata, commercialista, SPID. Research date 2026-10-27. Most sources are Italian professional press (fisco7, ecnews, fiscoetasse, money.it, informazionefiscale, partitaiva.it, pasqualepillitteri.it); official AdE pages are cited where I fetched them. Dates I worked out from the calendar myself (weekend/holiday shifts) are labelled "computed".

## 1. The 2026-27 F24 calendar and annual returns for this business

### Takeaway
Gennaro has three kinds of F24 deadline. Every 16th he pays ritenute on wages (and IVA too if he liquidates monthly). Income tax falls in summer and on 30 Nov. IVA has its own dates: Q3 (if quarterly), acconto 28 Dec 2026, saldo 16 Mar 2027. The annual returns are Redditi PF + ISA and the 770 (both due 2 Nov 2026), CU to employees (16 Mar), and dichiarazione IVA (30 Apr). IRAP does not apply to a ditta individuale. With 3 employees he almost certainly cannot use the forfettario.

### Cited Findings
**Monthly (16th of each month, F24)**
- On the 16th of each month: IVA for the previous month (monthly liquidators), IRPEF ritenute on employee and self-employed income, and INPS contributions for employees. Example: 16 Oct 2026 = IVA of September + ritenute + INPS dipendenti. — [fisco7, scadenzario ottobre 2026](https://www.fisco7.it/2026/09/lo-scadenzario-fiscale-di-ottobre-2026/)
- 16 Nov 2026 (Mon): IVA of October (monthly); IVA Q3 for quarterly filers, with 1% interest; third instalment of INPS fixed contributions (minimale) for artigiani/commercianti, forfettari included. — [pasqualepillitteri.it, novembre 2026](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/news/23939/scadenze-fiscali-novembre-2026-partite-iva-forfettari); same core list in [partitaiva.it calendario 2026](https://www.partitaiva.it/scadenze-fiscali-2026-calendario/)
- The fisco7 October calendar does not name the codice tributo for ritenute (1001); see Inferences. — [fisco7](https://www.fisco7.it/2026/09/lo-scadenzario-fiscale-di-ottobre-2026/)

**IVA: monthly or quarterly**
- Quarterly liquidation is allowed if the prior-year volume d'affari is at most €500,000 for service providers or €800,000 for other activities (raised by the Legge di Bilancio 2023 from €400k/€700k). Quarterly payments carry 1% interest. The option is made in the dichiarazione IVA (quadro VO, rigo VO2) and binds for at least one year. Forfettari do no periodic IVA. — [money.it, liquidazione IVA](https://www.money.it/liquidazione-iva-come-funziona-come-si-fa-versamento); [informazionefiscale, interessi trimestrali](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/interessi-trimestrali-IVA-dichiarazione-quadro-VL-23-36); [mysolution, rigo VO2 (10 Feb 2026)](https://www.mysolution.it/fisco/informazioni/news/2026/02/10/da-mensile-a-trimestrale-come-esercitare-lopzione-nel-rigo-vo2/). One guide still quotes €400k/€700k and is outdated: [fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/iva-trimestrale-quali-sono-le-scadenze/).
- Acconto IVA 2026: due 28 Dec 2026, because 27 Dec is a Sunday. Codes: 6013 (monthly), 6035 (quarterly). Historical method = 88% of the December liquidation (monthly) or of the prior-year annual-return debt (quarterly). Not due if ≤ €103.29. — [Assolombarda, acconto IVA](https://www.assolombarda.it/servizi/fisco/scadenze/acconto-iva-versamento-5); [ecnews, determinazione acconto](https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/?p=136812). Finom says 29 Dec 2026 ([finom](https://finom.co/it-it/blog/acconto-iva/)), which looks wrong.
- LIPE (comunicazione liquidazioni periodiche IVA) for Q3 2026: due 30 Nov 2026. — [fisco7](https://www.fisco7.it/2026/09/lo-scadenzario-fiscale-di-ottobre-2026/)
- Saldo IVA from the annual return: due 16 March each year if over €10.33. It can be paid later with 0.40% a month or in instalments. The dichiarazione IVA filed on its own is due by 30 April (2026 rules). — [informazionefiscale, scadenze marzo 2026](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/scadenze-fiscali-marzo-2026-rottamazione-cu-iva); [fiscoetasse, saldo IVA](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/approfondimenti/14687-dichiarazione-iva-termini-e-modalita-di-versamento-del-saldo-iva.html)
- The Q4 LIPE can be merged into the annual dichiarazione IVA if that is filed by the end of February (art. 21-bis DL 78/2010). — [fiscomania, novità LIPE](https://fiscomania.com/novita-intrastat-lipe-dichiarazione-iva/)

**Income taxes (IRPEF + addizionali), ISA, CPB**
- 2026 summer: DL 89/2026 (in force 23 May 2026) moved to 20 July 2026, with no surcharge, the payments from Redditi/IRAP/IVA returns normally due by 30 June 2026. It applies to ISA subjects, forfettari and soci of transparent entities. The reason: the ISA software only came out on 13 May 2026. Sources disagree on the surcharge for paying 21 Jul–20 Aug (0.40% vs 0.80%). — [centrofiscale](https://centrofiscale.com/proroga-versamenti-dichiarazioni-fiscali-2026-20-luglio-isa-forfettari/); [cafinforma](https://cafinforma.it/proroga-forfettari-2026-scadenza-20-luglio/); [informazionefiscale](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/dichiarazione-redditi-2026-scadenza-imposte-partite-iva-dipendenti-pensionati)
- 30 Nov 2026 (Mon): second acconto IRPEF (forfettari: second acconto of the imposta sostitutiva, code 1791). The same day: second acconto of INPS contributions on income above the minimale for artigiani/commercianti. — [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/news/23939/scadenze-fiscali-novembre-2026-partite-iva-forfettari); [partitaiva.it](https://www.partitaiva.it/scadenze-fiscali-2026-calendario/)
- 16 Dec 2026: last instalment for anyone who chose to spread saldo + primo acconto in instalments. — [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/news/23939/scadenze-fiscali-novembre-2026-partite-iva-forfettari)
- Modello Redditi 2026 (PF), IRAP and Modello 770/2026: due 2 Nov 2026, because 31 Oct is a Saturday and 1 Nov a holiday. Adesione to the concordato preventivo biennale 2026-2027 has the same deadline, "termine differito dalla L. 88/2026". CPB is only for ISA subjects; forfettari are excluded. — [fisco7](https://www.fisco7.it/2026/09/lo-scadenzario-fiscale-di-ottobre-2026/); [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/news/23939/scadenze-fiscali-novembre-2026-partite-iva-forfettari)
- ISA compliance letters for tax year 2024 are being sent under provv. prot. 208057 of 14 Jul 2026. — [search summary of redigo/ecnews results; provvedimento PDF](https://www.redigo.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Provvedimento-Cassetto-fiscale_16-luglio-2026.pdf) (prot. 208057 seen only in a search snippet, not opened)

**IRAP**
- Since tax year 2022, persone fisiche running a business or a profession are outside IRAP (L. 234/2021, art. 1 c. 8). This holds even with employees: an AdE answer says no IRAP for imprese individuali, imprese familiari included. Circolare 4/E of 18 Feb 2022 gives the rules. — [arlettipartners](https://arlettipartners.com/it/abolizione-irap-2022-persone-fisiche-partita-iva/); [NT+ Fisco, Sole 24 Ore](https://ntplusfisco.ilsole24ore.com/art/esenti-dall-irap-anche-imprese-familiari-AEPsTxAB); [ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/esclusione-irap-per-professionisti-ed-imprenditori-individuali/)

**Certificazione Unica**
- CU to employees and to AdE (for the precompilata): due 16 March. In 2026 it fell on 16 Mar 2026. — [informazionefiscale, marzo 2026](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/scadenze-fiscali-marzo-2026-rottamazione-cu-iva); [money.it marzo 2026](https://www.money.it/scadenze-fiscali-marzo-2026-tutte-le-date-da-non-dimenticare)

**Naples addizionale comunale IRPEF**
- Under the Patto per Napoli (Draghi–Manfredi, 2022), Comune delibera 503 raised the addizionale 0.1 points from 2023 and another 0.1 from 2024. It was 0.8% (exempt below €8,000) up to 2022, 0.9% in 2023 and 1.0% from 2024, with the exemption threshold raised to €12,000. — [napolike](https://www.napolike.it/napoli-nel-2023-aumenta-irpef-ecco-di-quanto-e-perche); [fanpage, Patto per Napoli](https://www.fanpage.it/napoli/patto-per-napoli-draghi-scheda/); [fanpage, aumenti Irpef](https://www.fanpage.it/napoli/napoli-dal-2023-tassa-di-1-euro-a-capodichino-e-aumenti-irpef-da-13-a-628-euro-a-famiglia/)
- A third-party calculator gives Naples 1.0% flat, exempt up to €12,000, for 2026, but labels the figure "fallback 2025" (not a confirmed 2026 delibera). — [tuttocalcolato](https://tuttocalcolato.it/calcolo/irpef/napoli/)

**Forfettario: what changes**
- 2026 limits: ricavi ≤ €85,000. Between €85,001 and €100,000 you leave the regime the following 1 January; above €100,000 you leave at once. Personnel costs (employees, collaborators, etc.) ≤ €20,000 gross. Employment/pension income ≤ €35,000 for 2026 only (L. 199/2025, art. 1 c. 27; back to €30,000 from 2027 unless extended). — [fisco7, forfettari 2026](https://www.fisco7.it/2026/01/legge-di-bilancio-forfettari-le-novita-del-2026/); [partitaiva.it, forfettario](https://www.partitaiva.it/?p=9240)
- Forfettari pay no periodic IVA ([money.it](https://www.money.it/liquidazione-iva-come-funziona-come-si-fa-versamento)), pay the imposta sostitutiva acconti (code 1791), and owe €2 bollo on e-invoices over €77.47 without IVA. The Q3 bollo is due 30 Nov 2026; AdE shows the amount in Fatture e Corrispettivi by 15 Nov. If Q1+Q2 bollo is at most €5,000 it can be paid with Q3 by 30 Nov. — [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/news/23939/scadenze-fiscali-novembre-2026-partite-iva-forfettari). Sources disagree on the Q3 bollo date: Qonto says 16 Nov ([qonto](https://qonto.com/it/resources/template/fiscal-deadlines)).
- Forfettari are excluded from the CPB. — [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/news/23939/scadenze-fiscali-novembre-2026-partite-iva-forfettari)

### Inferences
- **Forfettario is effectively ruled out.** Three employees at a pizzeria will cost far more than €20,000 gross a year, so Gennaro fails the personnel-cost test. The forfettario notes only matter if the business shrinks (e.g. one part-time helper).
- **Remaining calendar from today (2026-10-27), computed** (16th or end of month, weekend shifts applied; check against the AdE calendar):
  - 2 Nov 2026 (Mon): Redditi PF 2026 with ISA, Modello 770/2026, CPB adesione.
  - 16 Nov 2026 (Mon): IVA Oct (monthly) or Q3 (quarterly, code 6033 + 1%); ritenute on October wages (code 1001); INPS dipendenti; INPS artigiani/commercianti Q3 fissi.
  - 30 Nov 2026 (Mon): second acconto IRPEF (code 4034); LIPE Q3; INPS acconto above minimale; POS–RT link deadline for September POS activations (§2).
  - 16 Dec 2026 (Wed): IVA Nov (monthly); ritenute on November wages, usually including tredicesima-related withholding; any IMU saldo if he owns the premises.
  - 28 Dec 2026 (Mon): acconto IVA (6013/6035).
  - 31 Dec 2026 (Thu): POS–RT link deadline for October activations.
  - 18 Jan 2027 (Mon; 16 Jan is a Saturday): IVA Dec (monthly); ritenute on December wages.
  - 16 Feb 2027 (Tue): IVA Jan; ritenute Jan; INPS artigiani/commercianti Q4 fissi.
  - 1 Mar 2027 (Mon; 28 Feb is a Sunday): LIPE Q4, unless it goes into a dichiarazione IVA filed by then.
  - 16 Mar 2027 (Tue): saldo IVA 2026 (code 6099; quarterly filers settle Q4 here); CU 2027 to the 3 employees and to AdE.
  - 30 Apr 2027 (Fri): dichiarazione IVA 2027.
  - 30 Jun 2027 (Wed): saldo IRPEF 2026 + primo acconto 2027 + addizionali. The 2025 and 2026 precedent is a July proroga for ISA subjects.
  - 2 Nov 2027 (Tue; 31 Oct 2027 is a Sunday, 1 Nov a holiday): Redditi PF 2027 and 770/2027.
- Usual codici tributo, from general knowledge, not checked this pass: 1001 for ritenute on wages; 3802 for addizionale regionale withheld; 3848 (saldo) and 3847 (acconto) for addizionale comunale withheld; 6001–6012 for monthly IVA; 4033/4034 for IRPEF acconti; 4001 for saldo.
- The commercialista and the payroll consultant (consulente del lavoro, often the same studio) normally prepare every F24 and return. Gennaro "finds out" from the studio's F24 sheets, not from AdE. There is no official push notice of a due payment (§5).
- Somministrazione in a pizzeria is a service for IVA purposes (IVA usually 10%), so the €500,000 quarterly threshold, not €800,000, most likely applies. I did not confirm this against a source.
- ISA for restaurants/pizzerias has its own model code; I did not identify it.

### Gaps
- The 2026 official AdE scadenzario page could not be fetched (old URL returns 404). All dates come from press calendars.
- The final DL 89/2026 surcharge (0.40% vs 0.80%) and whether ISA subjects had a 2026 instalment plan to 16 Dec are not confirmed.

- Whether "L. 88/2026" really moved the CPB deadline is reported by fisco7 only.
- No source gives 2027 dates. The 2027 list is computed from the 2026 rules.

## 2. Fatturazione elettronica, corrispettivi telematici, the 2026 POS–RT link, IVA precompilata

### Takeaway
Every day the registratore telematico (RT) records and sends the takings. Gennaro's B2B invoices (rare at a pizzeria) go through SdI. New in 2026: every POS must be linked once to the RT in Fatture e Corrispettivi → "Gestione collegamenti". This is web UI only, by the owner or a delegate. The link must be redone whenever a POS or RT changes, by the end of the second month after the change. Fines run €1,000–4,000 plus licence suspension. The IVA precompilata (draft registers, LIPE, annual return) is still experimental for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **POS–RT legal basis and provvedimento:** Legge di Bilancio 2025 (L. 207/2024, art. 1, commi 74-77, amending art. 2 c. 3 D.Lgs. 127/2015), implemented by AdE Provvedimento n. 424470 of 31 Oct 2025. It applies to transactions from 1 Jan 2026. The link is virtual: the RT serial number is associated with each payment instrument's identifier. — [money.it](https://www.money.it/collegamento-obbligatorio-tra-pos-e-registratore-di-cassa-le-imprese-rischiano-sanzioni-fino-a-eur4000); [ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/fisco-e-patrimonio/imposte-sul-reddito/pos-e-scontrini-obbligo-di-collegamento-dal-1-gennaio-2026/); [leggioggi](https://www.leggioggi.it/pos-registratore-di-cassa-collegati-dal-2026/); [fiscoetasse (provvedimento PDF link)](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1189-collegamento-cassa-e-pos-istruzioni-per-provvedere-dal-5-marzo.html)
- **Service and path:** reserved area of "Fatture e Corrispettivi", function "Gestione collegamenti" for RT users. Users of the web procedure "Documento Commerciale online" do it inside that procedure. Login is SPID/CIE/CNS or Entratel/Fisconline. Delegated intermediaries can do it, through the "Accreditamento e censimento dispositivi" service. The system pre-lists the payment instruments in the merchant's name, as reported by the payment operators. — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1189-collegamento-cassa-e-pos-istruzioni-per-provvedere-dal-5-marzo.html); [leggioggi](https://www.leggioggi.it/pos-registratore-di-cassa-collegati-dal-2026/)
- **Deadlines:** the service opened 5 Mar 2026. POS in use by Jan 2026: by 20 Apr 2026 (45 days). POS activated or changed later: between the 6th and the last day of the second month after activation. Feb → 6–30 Apr; Mar → 6–31 May; Apr → 6–30 Jun; Jul → 6–30 Sep; Aug → 6–31 Oct; Sep → 6–30 Nov 2026; Oct → 6–31 Dec 2026. The link must be updated when a POS moves to another RT, or when an RT or POS is added or retired. — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1189-collegamento-cassa-e-pos-istruzioni-per-provvedere-dal-5-marzo.html); [money.it (28 Sep 2026)](https://www.money.it/pos-e-registratore-telematico-obbligo-entro-il-31-luglio)
- **Sanctions:** €100 per omitted or wrong transmission, capped at €1,000 a quarter; €1,000–4,000 (the penalty for not installing the RT) for a missing link; accessory suspension of licence or authorisation for 15 days to 2 months. Trade associations sought lighter penalties for formal errors via the DL fiscale; unconfirmed. — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1189-collegamento-cassa-e-pos-istruzioni-per-provvedere-dal-5-marzo.html); [money.it (28 Sep 2026)](https://www.money.it/pos-e-registratore-telematico-obbligo-entro-il-31-luglio)
- Unjustified POS terminals attributable to the merchant expose them to "pesanti sanzioni" (paywalled teaser). — [mysolution, Mar 2026](https://www.mysolution.it/fisco/approfondimenti/commenti/2026/03/pos-collegamento-rt/)
- **IVA precompilata 2026:** Provv. n. 42054 of 3 Feb 2026 extends the experiment to 2026 operations with unchanged access rules. AdE puts in the reserved area drafts of the IVA registers (artt. 23 and 25 DPR 633/72), the LIPE and the annual dichiarazione IVA. The drafts are built from e-invoices, cross-border data and corrispettivi telematici. Delegated intermediaries can access them. AdE has announced a possible wider audience from 2027. Fiscomania describes the audience as quarterly liquidators resident in Italy. — [informazionefiscale](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/dichiarazione-iva-2026-lipe-registri-precompilata-novita); [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1674-iva-lipe-e-registri-la-precompilata-iva-2026.html); [provvedimento PDF](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/files/21236/provvedimento-n-42054-precompilata-iva-2026.pdf); [fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/novita-intrastat-lipe-dichiarazione-iva/)
- **Fatture e Corrispettivi access and download:** since 2019, viewing and downloading e-invoices needs a "consultazione" opt-in by the taxpayer or a specifically delegated intermediary. Bulk download of issued, received and "messe a disposizione" invoices exists in the portal. The delega unica (Provv. 2 Oct 2024) covers "consultazione e acquisizione delle fatture elettroniche" and "consultazione dei dati rilevanti ai fini IVA". — [informazionefiscale, deleghe](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/deleghe-fattura-elettronica-corrispettivi-telematici-novita-istruzioni-agenzia-entrate); [edotto, download massivo](https://edotto.com/articolo/fatture-elettroniche-download-massivo-codice-contrattuale-in-mancanza-del-cf); [ANCE guide, delega unica](https://ance.it/wp-content/uploads/allegati/La_delega_unica_per_l'utilizzo_dei_servizi_online.pdf)

### Inferences
- What Gennaro actually does, from general knowledge not checked this pass: daily closure on the RT, which stores and sends the corrispettivi (statutory limit 12 days from the operation). Any fattura (e.g. a catering client) goes through SdI within 12 days, normally via the gestionale or commercialista. Monthly: nothing beyond F24, unless he self-checks the corrispettivi totals in the portal.
- **Data AdE now holds:** POS transactions reported by payment operators, linked RT, daily takings. AdE can match card takings against corrispettivi automatically. Any mismatch is likely to turn up later as a compliance letter (§4). A third-party tool cannot see this match, except through the delegated portal.
- **Programmatic access:** the POS link is web UI only (owner or delegate). Fatture e Corrispettivi offers bulk download through a logged-in web UI only. Receiving invoices as a machine feed is possible by giving a codice destinatario / accredited SdI channel (SdICoop / SFTP) to a provider, which is how gestionali do it. No AdE API was found for corrispettivi or the precompilata IVA.

### Gaps
- The official AdE POS–RT guide (19 Feb 2026) and FAQ (25 Mar 2026) are cited by fiscoetasse but were not fetched. The exact sanction articles (art. 11 D.Lgs. 471/97 as amended) are not confirmed.
- Whether the DL fiscale changed POS-link sanctions in 2026 is unconfirmed.
- Whether a monthly-liquidating ordinario business is inside the 2026 IVA precompilata audience is unclear.

## 3. The cassetto fiscale: contents, freshness, delega, APIs, F24 export

### Takeaway
The cassetto fiscale is the official "done" record. It holds tax returns (11 years, with processing status), every F24/F23 paid (with quietanze and telematic direct debits, whatever the channel), refunds, registered acts, ISA data, and the section "L'Agenzia scrive" (avvisi, compliance letters). It is web UI for the owner with SPID. Intermediaries reach it through the Entratel "cassetto fiscale delegato", now under the delega unica (since 8 Dec 2025). As of July 2026 AdE has started real API access ("Catalogo dei servizi di interoperabilità", Provv. 200918 of 6 Jul 2026). Only bulk CU download is live, for delegated intermediaries. F24 and declaration data by API are not yet announced.

### Cited Findings
- **Sections (AdE official guide):**
  - Anagrafica (sedi, attività, legal representatives; P.IVA QR code).
  - Dichiarazioni fiscali: last 11 years, with processing status of each model.
  - ISA/Studi di settore: precalculated ISA data as XML.
  - Comunicazioni: condoni, CPB, dichiarazioni d'intento, leasing/nautica.
  - Rimborsi: since 1994, plus automated IVA refunds.
  - Versamenti: F24 searchable by period within a year, F24 quietanze, F23, "disposizioni di pagamento con addebito telematico".
  - Dati del Registro: acts since 1986.
  - Cassetto of incorporated companies.
  - The guide does not mention any PDF/CSV export for versamenti; it mentions ISA XML, printable act details and return receipts. Guide last updated 25 Sep 2025, with no statement of data freshness.
  — [AdE, Guida consultazioni del cassetto fiscale](https://assistenza.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/guida-consultazioni-cassetto-fiscale)
- **Delega (traditional):** up to two intermediaries. It can be given online, at an office (signed PDF), or handed to the intermediary with an ID document. In the last case an activation code is posted to the domicilio fiscale within 15 days for the intermediary. Revocable any time. The intermediary needs Entratel and adherence to the "Cassetto Fiscale Delegato" regolamento. — [AdE, delega consultazione cassetto](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/web/guest/delega-per-la-consultazione-del-cassetto-fiscale/infogen-delega-per-la-consultazione-del-cassetto-fiscale); [AdE, Deleghe intermediari (assistenza)](https://assistenza.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/deleghe-intermediari); [AdE form, Delega/revoca](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/449236/Delega+revoca+per+la+consultazione+del+cassetto+fiscale_Delega_revoca_cassFisc+EDIT.PDF/349ced94-5fee-4adb-f3e1-ebbd2d2f11dc); [studiocataldi](https://www.studiocataldi.it/articoli/43542-cassetto-fiscale-a-cosa-serve-e-chi-vi-puo-accedere.asp)
- **Delega unica (from 8 Dec 2025):** one communication delegates services on both the AdE and AdE-Riscossione sites.
  - AdE services: cassetto fiscale delegato; e-invoice consultation and acquisition; IVA data; e-invoicing and storage; device registration; ISA; CPB data.
  - AdE-Riscossione services (Equipro): debts, payment plans, rate up to €120,000.
  - Valid until 31 Dec of the 4th year after conferral; renewable from 2 Oct of the last year.
  - Old deleghe active on 5 Dec 2025 last until their own expiry but **no later than 28 Feb 2027**.
  - The taxpayer confers it from their own area riservata (SPID/CIE/CNS) at "Il tuo profilo → Deleghe → Intermediari → Nuova delega", or the intermediary submits it digitally after a signed agreement.
  — [AdE, comunicato stampa 9 Dec 2025](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/-/comunicato-stampa-del-9-dicembre-2025); [AdE assistenza deleghe](https://assistenza.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/deleghe-intermediari)
- **The taxpayer can see audit data:** the list of intermediaries who downloaded their CU appears in the cassetto. — [AdE, search summary of assistenza pages](https://assistenza.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/deleghe-intermediari)
- **API / interoperability:**
  - Provv. n. 200918 of 6 Jul 2026 (implements art. 23 D.Lgs. 1/2024) sets up bulk, structured acquisition of cassetto data, acts and communications. This includes AdE-Riscossione cartelle/ruoli tied to AdE assessment acts.

### Inferences
- **"Done" for F24:** the Versamenti section shows every F24 paid, including via home banking, because all F24s are reported to AdE whatever the channel. The owner can see it with SPID. Export is view/print only in the web UI; no CSV was found. Through the commercialista's Entratel, the same data is in the cassetto delegato.
- **"Due" for F24:** the cassetto does not show amounts still to pay. The "due" side lives only in the commercialista's working papers until AdE issues an avviso (§4). After that, AdE-Riscossione shows debts and payment plans (in the delega unica scope).
- **Programmatic readability:**
  - Returns, versamenti, rimborsi: owner web UI, intermediary web UI (cassetto delegato).
  - CU: intermediary API (live since Jul 2026).
  - Other cassetto data: API announced under Provv. 200918, not yet live per the sources found.
  - A third-party tool gets nothing directly, unless it is or acts through an enabled intermediary. Screen-scraping the owner's SPID session is the only owner-side route.
- Practical risk for Gennaro: if his commercialista's delega predates 8 Dec 2025, it lapses by 28 Feb 2027 at the latest and must be re-conferred under the delega unica.

## 4. Lettere di compliance and avvisi bonari (36-bis / 54-bis / 36-ter): channel and deadlines

### Takeaway
For avvisi bonari prepared from 1 Jan 2025, Gennaro has 60 days to pay with a reduced penalty or to object. If the notice went to his commercialista via Entratel, the 60 days become 90, counted from transmission to the intermediary. Notices and compliance letters appear in the cassetto fiscale under "L'Agenzia scrive". That section was widened in July 2026 to include certain liquidation and automated assessment notices.

### Cited Findings
- D.Lgs. 108/2024 extends the deadline from 30 to 60 days. It covers both payment and the answer with observations/documents. It applies to automated checks (art. 36-bis DPR 600/73, art. 54-bis DPR 633/72) and formal checks (art. 36-ter), for communications prepared from 1 Jan 2025. — [fisco7](https://www.fisco7.it/2024/09/avvisi-bonari-il-termine-per-il-pagamento-raddoppia-da-30-a-60-giorni/); [ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/accertamento-e-contenzioso/accertamento/avvisi-bonari-dall1-1-2025-entro-60-giorni-difesa-o-pagamento/)
- If the communication goes to the intermediary: 90 days from transmission to the intermediary (before: 60 + 30). Tassazione separata stays at 30 days. — [ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/?p=12622)
- Paying on time cuts penalties to one third (automated checks) or two thirds (formal checks). — [businessonline](https://www.businessonline.it/news/avvisi-bonari-dellagenzia-delle-entrate-le-2-modifiche-positive-al-via-ora_n76044.html)
- Notices can be read in the cassetto at "L'Agenzia scrive", where one can also pay or ask for assistance (CIVIS). — [investireoggi](https://www.investireoggi.it/avvisi-bonari-nel-2025-piu-tempo-per-pagare-la-prima-o-unica-rata/)
- Summer suspension: response and payment terms were frozen in August, with restart on 5 Sep 2025, though acts could still be notified. One source only. — [quotidianopiu](https://www.quotidianopiu.it/dettaglio/12293097/avvisi-bonari-dal-5-settembre-2025-ripartono-termini-e-scadenze)
- Provv. 19 Nov 2024 n. 419815 governs consultation and management in the area riservata of the 36-bis/54-bis communications. — [provvedimento PDF](https://dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Provvedimento-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-19-novembre-2024-n.-419815.pdf)
- Provv. prot. 210791 of 16 Jul 2026 (art. 23 D.Lgs. 1/2024) widens "L'Agenzia scrive" to include certain liquidation notices, partial automated assessment notices and related self-review acts. The go-live date was to be announced; one secondary source says 28 Jul 2026. — [provvedimento PDF (redigo)](https://www.redigo.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Provvedimento-Cassetto-fiscale_16-luglio-2026.pdf); [finanzaefisco](https://www.finanzaefisco.com/category/agenzia-delle-entrate-2/)
- AdE's web service for choosing a PEC domicilio digitale for "atti, avvisi e altra corrispondenza" (D.Lgs. 13/2024; live March 2025) is only for persone fisiche and for professionals/entities not required to register in albi or registri. — [ecnews (19 Mar 2025)](https://www.ecnews.it/lavoro/news-del-giorno/disponibile-servizio-indicare-domicilio-digitale-comunicazioni-agenzia-entrate/)
- AdE's 2026-2028 plan reportedly makes app IO the main channel. An IO message would only alert the taxpayer and send them to the area riservata / cassetto to read the actual letter (news report, not official). — [Libero (Feb 2026)](https://www.liberoquotidiano.it/news/economia/46366148/fisco-smartphone-novita-chi-rischia-grosso/)

### Inferences
- As a ditta individuale, Gennaro has a mandatory PEC in the Registro Imprese / INI-PEC. AdE sends formal communications to the business there, which is why the persone-fisiche domicilio service doesn't apply to his business role. Not confirmed by a source fetched here.
- Practical channels for the "due" signal: Entratel to the commercialista (90-day clock), the business PEC (60 days), or raccomandata. In every case a copy is in the cassetto ("L'Agenzia scrive"), web UI. The PEC is the only owner-side machine-readable channel (IMAP). The cassetto copy may become API-readable under Provv. 200918, not confirmed.
- Compliance letters (e.g. ISA, or likely future POS-vs-corrispettivi mismatches) are invitations to ravvedimento, with no fixed payment deadline.

### Gaps
- No official AdE page fetched confirming the PEC channel for 36-bis notices to imprese, or the August suspension rule for 2026.
- Go-live of the widened "L'Agenzia scrive" (28 Jul 2026) is from one secondary source only.

## 5. Is there an official personal tax calendar for a partita IVA?

### Takeaway
No source found shows a personal, per-P.IVA "scadenzario" from AdE. AdE publishes a general calendar. Personal "due" items arrive only after the fact (avvisi, compliance letters, cassetto "L'Agenzia scrive"), and app IO messages are planned or partial and only alert.

### Cited Findings
- AdE planned (PIAO 2024-2026) app IO messages on tax topics and deadlines, described as both general and personalised, with PagoPA, starting from 2024. The only condition mentioned is having app IO installed. — [informazionefiscale (25 Mar 2024)](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/notifiche-agenzia-delle-entrate-app-io-scadenze-novita); [AdE press release, App IO (10 Apr 2024)](https://agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/6022020/014_Com.+st.+Messaggi+Agenzia+Entrate_App+IO+10.04.2024/4a3f6269-f7c7-6c26-e7cb-c081420d06b9)
- The 2026-2028 plan names app IO the main channel for notices, reminders and compliance letters, but only as an alert pointing to the area riservata. — [Libero (Feb 2026)](https://www.liberoquotidiano.it/news/economia/46366148/fisco-smartphone-novita-chi-rischia-grosso/)
- The cassetto shows a computed figure only for refunds and processed returns, not upcoming amounts due. — [AdE guida consultazioni](https://assistenza.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/guida-consultazioni-cassetto-fiscale)

### Inferences
