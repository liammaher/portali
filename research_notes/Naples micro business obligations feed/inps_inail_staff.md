# Social security, insurance and employee obligations of a Naples micro-business (pizzeria, ditta individuale, 3 employees), 2026

Persona: Gennaro, pizzeria in Naples, ditta individuale, iscritto INPS Gestione Commercianti, 3 employees (CCNL Pubblici Esercizi or Turismo), consulente del lavoro + commercialista, SPID. Research date: 2026-10-27. Research budget was about 25 tool calls, so several points are marked unconfirmed. Most sources are specialist press summarising the official texts; INPS/INAIL primary pages were mostly not readable through the fetch tool (inps.it pages came back as navigation only).

## 1. INPS Gestione Commercianti (the owner's own contributions) and the Cassetto previdenziale artigiani e commercianti

### Takeaway
For 2026 (INPS circolare n. 14 of 9 Feb 2026) a commerciante owes €4,611.64 fixed contributions on the €18,808 minimale, in four F24 installments of about €1,152.91 (18 May, 20 Aug, 16 Nov 2026, 16 Feb 2027), plus percentage contributions on income above the minimale, paid with the IRPEF saldo/acconti. The precompiled F24 data, payments, estratto conto and debts sit in the Cassetto previdenziale artigiani e commercianti, which the owner reads with SPID/CIE/CNS or an intermediary reads with a delega. It is a logged-in web UI only: no public API was found.

### Cited Findings
- Circolare INPS n. 14 of 9 February 2026 sets the 2026 values. Minimale 2026: €18,808 (up 1.4% for inflation) — [ecnews, 10 Feb 2026](https://www.ecnews.it/lavoro/imposte-contributi-e-premi/contributi-e-premi/artigiani-commercianti-valori-contributivi-anno-2026/); [dottrinalavoro](https://www.dottrinalavoro.it/notizie-c/inps-gestioni-artigiani-e-commercianti-i-contributi-per-il-2026)

One secondary table showed the higher rates without the threshold; ecnews gives the threshold version — [ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/lavoro/imposte-contributi-e-premi/contributi-e-premi/artigiani-commercianti-valori-contributivi-anno-2026/); [partitaiva.it](https://www.partitaiva.it/contributi-inps-artigiani-commercianti-2026/)
- Fixed contribution on the minimale, commercianti (titolari and coadiuvanti): €4,611.64/year (€4,604.20 IVS incl. the indennizzo per cessazione funding + €7.44 maternità); €384.31 per month for part years. Artigiani: €4,521.36 — [ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/lavoro/imposte-contributi-e-premi/contributi-e-premi/artigiani-commercianti-valori-contributivi-anno-2026/)
- Quarterly installments: €1,152.91 (commercianti), €1,130.34 (artigiani) — [search summary of partitaiva.it / money.it](https://www.partitaiva.it/contributi-inps-artigiani-commercianti-2026/)

A late applicant must file by 28 Feb 2027 for it to apply from 2027. It is an alternative to the 50% reduction for 2025 first-time registrants, not cumulative with it — [fisco7, Feb 2026](https://www.fisco7.it/2026/02/forfettari-domanda-inps-per-lo-sconto-contributivo-2026-scadenza-perentoria-il-28-febbraio/); [Fiscal Focus, 25 Feb 2026](https://www.fiscal-focus.it/news-24/ore-21-03-forfettari-artigiani-e-commercianti-domanda-al-regime-contributivo-agevolato-inps-entro-il-28-febbraio-2026,3,182113); [informazionefiscale](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/riduzione-contributi-inps-forfettari-regime-agevolazioni)

- INPS official simulator for artigiani and commercianti online since 3 July 2026 (messaggio n. 2254/2026). Secondary source, unverified on inps.it — [investireoggi / search summary](https://www.investireoggi.it/riduzione-contributi-regime-forfettario-2026-domanda-inps-tempi-e-procedura-passo-per-passo/)

INPS points the payer there rather than mailing F24s — [ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/lavoro/imposte-contributi-e-premi/contributi-e-premi/artigiani-commercianti-valori-contributivi-anno-2026/); [pensionioggi](https://www.pensionioggi.it/notizie/lavoro/artigiani-e-commercianti-disponibili-i-modelli-f24-per-il-versamento-dei-contributi-876786); [fiscoetasse 2018](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/rassegna-stampa/24582-f24-artigiani-e-commercianti-nel-cassetto-previdenziale.html)

- Access: the holder with SPID/CIE/CNS, or an intermediary with a valid delega (direct delega from the holder, or "indirect" delega through intermediaries or professional bodies that have agreements with INPS: consulenti del lavoro, commercialisti, associazioni di categoria) — [INPS messaggio 1792 of 29 Apr 2020](https://servizi2.inps.it/MessaggiZIP/Messaggio%20numero%201792%20del%2029-04-2020.pdf); [ilcommercialistaonline](https://www.ilcommercialistaonline.it/cassetto-previdenziale-inps/)
- INPS sends an email alert when the new F24 data are ready, to the holder or the delegated intermediary, where INPS has an email address on file — [INPS messaggio 1792/2020](https://servizi2.inps.it/MessaggiZIP/Messaggio%20numero%201792%20del%2029-04-2020.pdf)
- INPS publishes 133 e-services on the PDND (Piattaforma Digitale Nazionale Dati) for interoperability between public bodies (messaggio 2171 of 7 Jul 2025). This channel is for administrations, not a private API to a contributor's Cassetto — [INPS news, Jul 2025](https://www.inps.it/it/it/inps-comunica/notizie/dettaglio-news-page.news.2025.07.piattaforma-digitale-nazionale-dati-i-servizi-erogati-dall-inps.html)

### Inferences
- **Forfettario probably doesn't apply to Gennaro.** Unverified in this session: the forfettario requires payroll costs of at most €20,000/year (L. 190/2014, c. 54). Three pizzeria employees almost certainly exceed that, so the 35% reduction is likely irrelevant and he pays the full €4,611.64 plus the percentage on income above €18,808. The report should say so, but check the rule.
- **How Gennaro learns what's due.** In practice the commercialista fetches the F24 data from the Cassetto under delega and pays or sends him the F24s. Gennaro learns of the dates from the commercialista, or from the INPS email alert if INPS has his address. The percentage contributions above the minimale are computed in Modello Redditi (quadro RR), so they show up only in the commercialista's tax computation, not as a precompiled F24.
- **Machine readability.** The record of "due" (Dati F24) and "done" (versamenti, estratto debitorio) is readable only in the logged-in web UI (owner via SPID, or intermediary via delega). No documented API for the contributor or a third-party tool was found. Third-party tools would need screen access through the owner's SPID session.

### Gaps
- **Not checked on inps.it:** the text of circolare 14/2026 (the inps.it pages returned only navigation), and whether quarterly amounts include the €7.44 maternity share split by quarter.
- **Update frequency:** I found no source on how often the Cassetto's payments and estratto conto refresh after an F24 is paid (practitioners commonly report a delay of weeks; unverified).
- **IRPEF dates for 2026:** the exact 2026 saldo/first-acconto (normally 30 June, or 30 July with +0.40%) and second-acconto (30 November) dates weren't confirmed here; the fiscal researcher should supply them.
- **2025 50% reduction:** its conditions for 2026 weren't researched.

## 2. Employees: payroll, F24, UniEmens, LUL, UniLav, CU/770, TFR, ferie; Cassetto previdenziale aziende

### Takeaway
The consulente del lavoro runs the monthly cycle. Each month: cedolino and LUL entries; F24 for INPS contributions and IRPEF ritenute (the 16th of the following month); the UniEmens flow (last day of the following month). For each hire, a UniLav by 24:00 the day before start; for a termination, within 5 days. Yearly: CU to employees and the Agenzia by 16 March, and the 770 around 31 Oct (2 Nov 2026). The employer's INPS position (matricola) can be seen in the Cassetto previdenziale del contribuente (aziende) by the employer, legal representatives or delegated intermediaries. It is a web UI, with no public read API found.

### Cited Findings
- UniLav for a hire: by 24:00 of the day before the job starts, even if that day is a holiday. For a termination (Cessazione section): within 5 days, moved to the next working day if the 5th is a holiday. In force-majeure urgency: a UniLav within 5 days after start; for urgent production needs: a UniUrg by 24:00 the day before, then the UniLav within 5 days — [Skello](https://www.skello.io/it/blog/modello-unilav-cose-e-come-funziona-la-comunicazione-obbligatoria-di-assunzione); [edotto](https://edotto.com/articolo/assunzioni-urgenti-come-utilizzare-il-modello-uniurg-per-i-lavoratori); [INPS page on comunicazioni obbligatorie](https://inps.it/it/it/inps-comunica/diritti-e-obblighi-in-materia-di-sicurezza-sociale-nell-unione-e/per-le-imprese/gestione-contratti-di-lavoro-invio-comunicazioni-obbligatorie.html)
- Late or missing UniLav: an administrative fine of €100–500 per worker (art. 19 c. 3 D.Lgs. 276/2003). Single secondary source. A late UniLav can also lose hiring incentives (sgravi) for the uncovered days — [money.it](https://www.money.it/assunzione-agevolate-e-comunicazione-unilav-errori-da-evitare)
- UniEmens monthly flow: due by the last day of the month after the month it covers (e.g. October by 30 November), moved to the next working day if that's a holiday. All private employers. INPS sometimes grants one-off extensions — [money.it](https://www.money.it/come-si-invia-una-denuncia-uniemens-all-inps); [fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/invio-uniemens-invio-tardivo/)
- CU 2026 (year 2025): to employees and to the Agenzia delle Entrate by 16 March 2026 (the CUs that feed the precompilata). One source says 17 March for delivery and 31 March for transmission — [investireoggi](https://www.investireoggi.it/cu-2026-scadenza-16-marzo-chi-deve-inviare-e-chi-deve-consegnare-entro-oggi/); [businessonline](https://www.businessonline.it/news/tutte-le-25-scadenze-fiscali-oggi-luned-16-marzo-chi-le-deva-pagare-e-adempiere-e-come-si-possono-ritardare-e-conseguenze_n83390.html)
- Modello 770/2026: sources conflict. One gives 31 October 2026, which is a Saturday, so it moves to Monday 2 November 2026; another says 31 July 2026 — [centrofiscale](https://centrofiscale.com/modello-770-2026-guida-scadenze-istruzioni/); [studiopizzano (CU/precompilata 2 Nov 2026)](https://www.studiopizzano.it/cu-2026-redditi-esenti-non-dichiarabili-precompilata-trasmissione-2-novembre-2026/)
- The Cassetto previdenziale del contribuente (aziende) is the single INPS channel where employers and their intermediaries check the main data of the company's contribution position. INPS defines its access rules, intermediaries included, in circolare 34 of 31 March 2023 — [INPS circolare 34/2023](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circolare-INPS-31-marzo-2023-n.-34.pdf); [INPS service page](https://www.inps.it/it/it/dettaglio-scheda.it.schede-servizio-strumento.schede-aree-tematiche.cassetto-previdenziale-del-contribuente-50240.cassetto-previdenziale-del-contribuente.html)
- Cassetti previdenziali have a "comunicazione bidirezionale" section: messages with the INPS office are logged and stay viewable in the Cassetto (shown for the domestic-work Cassetto, extended to intermediaries in 2018) — [INPS messaggio 2749/2018 via Ipsoa](https://www.ipsoa.it/documents/quotidiano/2018/07/07/lavoro-domestico-cassetto-previdenziale-accessibile-intermediari-abilitati)
- F24 INPS section for employees: codice sede, matricola INPS, period "da mm/aaaa" — [Agenzia delle Entrate risoluzione 36/E 2016](https://agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/303100/Risoluzione+n.+36+del+05+maggio+2016_Risoluzione+n.36E+del+05-05-2016.pdf/547bb5f8-7381-139e-66dd-785f13128b70)

### Inferences
- **Unconfirmed by a source this session:**
  - The F24 for employee INPS contributions and IRPEF ritenute is due on the 16th of the month after the pay month. This is long-standing general knowledge.
  - The LUL must be completed by the end of the month after the month it covers (art. 39 DL 112/2008).
  - TFR accrues monthly (retribuzione/13.5). Each employee chooses a pension fund or keeping the TFR with the employer. The Fondo Tesoreria INPS applies only to employers with 50+ employees, so not here.
  - Ferie: at least 4 weeks/year (D.Lgs. 66/2003), 2 of them within the year and the rest within 18 months. INPS checks contributions on untaken leave after the 18 months.
  - The cedolino payment date is set by the CCNL and the contract, not by law. A "by the 16th" rule for paying wages was not found.
- **What the owner actually sees:** the cedolini, the F24s the consulente sends (or that are debited on his account through the consulente's Entratel/home banking), the UniLav receipts, and the CU copies. The authoritative "done" record is the Cassetto previdenziale aziende (UniEmens received, F24 credited, DURC-relevant debts), readable by Gennaro with SPID as titolare, or by the consulente with a delega. UniLav records are on the regional/ministry CO system, which the consulente operates. On whether Gennaro can read his own CO history with SPID, see Gaps.
- **Third-party tools** (payroll software) hold their own copy. The official records are web-UI only for the owner. Intermediaries submit through web services and file uploads (UniEmens upload, CO via the regional portal), but no read API for the employer was found.

### Gaps
- **Pay timing:** CCNL Pubblici Esercizi (FIPE) and CCNL Turismo pay timing: which day the wage must be paid. Not researched.
- **Cassetto aziende:** its exact sections for 2026, and how fast F24 payments show there. Not found.
- **Read API:** whether INPS gives intermediaries a read web service for the Cassetto. Not found; PDND e-services are for public bodies.
- **Owner access to UniLav records:** whether the owner can see his own UniLav history (portal: Regione Campania "CO" / ClicLavoro) with SPID. Not researched.
- **Sanctions** for late UniEmens, LUL or F24 (ravvedimento operoso). Not researched.

## 3. INAIL: autoliquidazione and DURC online

### Takeaway
The INAIL premium for 2025/2026 was due 16 Feb 2026 in one payment, or in four installments chosen in the dichiarazione delle retribuzioni: 25% each on 16 Feb, 18 May, 20 Aug and 16 Nov 2026, with interest from the second installment. The dichiarazione delle retribuzioni was due 2 March 2026 through AL.P.I. online. The DURC is requested online on the INPS or INAIL portal (SPID/CIE/CNS) with the company's codice fiscale. It is valid 120 days, and the result arrives by PEC.

### Cited Findings
- Autoliquidazione 2025/2026: payment (single, or the first installment) due 16 February 2026. Four installments of 25% each: 16 Feb (no interest), 18 May, 20 Aug, 16 Nov 2026. Interest coefficients for installments 2–4: 0.00670548, 0.01363699, 0.02056849, from the 2025 average Treasury yield of 2.75% — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/3351-autoliquidazione-inail-20252026-scadenze-riduzioni-e-istruzioni-operative.html); [dottrinalavoro](https://www.dottrinalavoro.it/notizie-c/inail-autoliquidazione-2025-2026-pagamento-in-quattro-rate-del-premio); [ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/lavoro/news-del-giorno/autoliquidazione-2025-2026-pagamento-rateale-tasso-interesse-annuo-coefficienti/)
- The installment option is made in the dichiarazione delle retribuzioni. The dichiarazione delle retribuzioni 2025 was due 2 March 2026 (since 28 Feb was a Saturday), only through "AL.P.I. online" or "Invio telematico Dichiarazione Salari" — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/3351-autoliquidazione-inail-20252026-scadenze-riduzioni-e-istruzioni-operative.html)
- "Riduzione Presunto": an employer expecting lower 2026 wages had to send a reasoned notice by 16 Feb 2026 (service open from 2 Jan 2026; calculation and dichiarazioni from 8 Jan 2026) — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/3351-autoliquidazione-inail-20252026-scadenze-riduzioni-e-istruzioni-operative.html)
- From 1 Jan 2026 the "oscillazione in bonus" rate reductions apply provisionally (INAIL deliberazione 146 of 21 Jul 2025; circolare INAIL n. 3 of 20 Jan 2026): 14%–37% by headcount and ISAR value, 13% for non-significant PATs. They are subject to a later conguaglio and lost after serious safety violations in the last two years. Instructions cited: Istruzione operativa of 23 Dec 2025, plus the coefficients note of 8/9 Jan 2026 (sources give both dates). DPR 1124/1965 art. 28 — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/3351-autoliquidazione-inail-20252026-scadenze-riduzioni-e-istruzioni-operative.html); [dottrinalavoro](https://www.dottrinalavoro.it/notizie-c/inail-autoliquidazione-2025-2026-pagamento-in-quattro-rate-del-premio)
- DURC online: requested only through the INPS or INAIL portals, with SPID, CIE or CNS (old INAIL credentials invalid since 1 Oct 2021). The requester enters the codice fiscale of the company to check and a PEC for the result. One query covers INPS, INAIL and the Casse Edili (construction only). Valid up to 120 days, during which no new request can be made for the same subject. If irregular, the company gets a PEC and has at most 15 days to regularise. Basis: DL 34/2014 and DM 30 Jan 2015 — [fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/scaricare-durc-online/); [fiscozen verifica](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/verificare-durc-online/); [money.it](https://www.money.it/DURC-cos-e-come-richiederlo); [ANCE Enna 2015](https://enna.ance.it/2015/07/01/durc-on-line-da-oggi-in-vigore-la-nuova-procedura-telematica-di-richiesta-del-durc/?print=pdf)

### Inferences
- **The remaining installment.** The 4th installment (16 Nov 2026) is the next INAIL date after today (27 Oct 2026). The next autoliquidazione is 16 Feb 2027 (2026/2027).
- **What the owner sees.** Gennaro sees the premium only if he logs into the INAIL portal; the consulente normally computes it, files the dichiarazione and adds it to the 16 Feb F24. Unverified: INAIL makes the "basi di calcolo" (rates per PAT) available in the portal's company services.
- **When a pizzeria needs a DURC.** Typically for public incentives and grants, sgravi contributivi (the DURC regularity condition), public contracts, and some banking or subsidised finance. It is not needed for day-to-day operations. Anyone with SPID and an entitled role (the company itself, its consulente, a PA) can request it by codice fiscale. It is web UI plus PEC delivery. PAs check it through interoperability services; no public API for private requesters was found.

- **INAIL's own portal:** the name of its "Fascicolo azienda" section and any list of pending dues ("scadenze", "avvisi"). Not confirmed.
- **Machine access:** whether INAIL offers an API or cooperazione applicativa for private intermediaries. Not found.

## 4. Workplace safety (D.Lgs. 81/2008) for a pizzeria with 3 employees

### Takeaway
The new Accordo Stato-Regioni n. 59/CSR of 17 April 2025 (GU n. 119 of 24 May 2025) replaces the 2011 accords. It makes a 16-hour course mandatory for every datore di lavoro (6h refresh every 5 years; per secondary sources, within 24 months of entry into force). It adds a separate DL-SPP course for an employer acting as his own RSPP (8h common module + technical module; 8h refresh every 5 years). Worker training stays at 4h general + 4/8/12h specific with a 6h refresh every 5 years, now with a mandatory final test. Fire wardens refresh every 5 years (DM 2 Sept 2021). Missing the DVR costs arrest of 3–6 months or a fine of €3,559.60–9,112.57 after the 2023 +15.9% revaluation.

### Cited Findings
- Accordo Stato-Regioni approved 17 April 2025, published in GU n. 119 of 24 May 2025, in force from that date. It replaces the 21 Dec 2011 accords. Provisions take effect within one year, and within two years for the datore di lavoro courses (secondary sources) — [quotidianopiu](https://www.quotidianopiu.it/dettaglio/11493457/laccordo-stato-regioni-sulla-formazione-per-la-sicurezza-sul-lavoro); [dottrinalavoro](https://www.dottrinalavoro.it/notizie-c/articolo-accordo-stato-regioni-2025-formazione-per-la-sicurezza-sul-lavoro-e-nuovi-obblighi); [Clifford Chance briefing, Jun 2025](https://www.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2025/06/new-compulsory-training-on-health-and-safety-at-work-it.pdf)
- **Datore di lavoro** (new obligation for all employers): 16h course; refresh at least 6h every 5 years; e-learning allowed — [PuntoSicuro](https://www.puntosicuro.it/preposti-C-74/accordo-stato-regioni-2025-cambiano-le-regole-sui-corsi-a-tema-sicurezza-AR-25629/)
- **Datore di lavoro who is his own RSPP** (DL-SPP): an extra course on top of the 16h one. 8h common module (including a DVR-drafting exercise) + a technical module of 12 or 16h depending on the sector (agricoltura, pesca, costruzioni, chimico are named). The base course can't be e-learning. Refresh at least 8h every 5 years, e-learning allowed — [PuntoSicuro](https://www.puntosicuro.it/preposti-C-74/accordo-stato-regioni-2025-cambiano-le-regole-sui-corsi-a-tema-sicurezza-AR-25629/)
- **Workers:** 4h general + specific 4h (low risk) / 8h (medium) / 12h (high). Refresh at least 6h every 5 years. Mandatory final test for initial and refresher courses (new). E-learning allowed for general, low-risk specific and refresher; for medium/high risk only if a regional act allows it — [PuntoSicuro](https://www.puntosicuro.it/preposti-C-74/accordo-stato-regioni-2025-cambiano-le-regole-sui-corsi-a-tema-sicurezza-AR-25629/)
- **Preposti:** base 12h; refresh every 2 years, at least 6h; in presence or videoconference only. Preposti past the 2-year mark must refresh within 12 months of the Accordo's entry into force — [PuntoSicuro](https://www.puntosicuro.it/preposti-C-74/accordo-stato-regioni-2025-cambiano-le-regole-sui-corsi-a-tema-sicurezza-AR-25629/)
- **External RSPP/ASPP:** Modules A, B (now 5 sector modules) and C. Refresh 40h/5 years for an RSPP, 20h/5 years for an ASPP — [PuntoSicuro](https://www.puntosicuro.it/preposti-C-74/accordo-stato-regioni-2025-cambiano-le-regole-sui-corsi-a-tema-sicurezza-AR-25629/)
- **Addetti antincendio** (DM 2 settembre 2021, in force 4 Oct 2022, replacing DM 10/3/1998): refresh within 5 years of the last training. Refresher length 2, 5 or 8h by risk level. Those already past 5 years on 4 Oct 2022 had until 4 Oct 2023 — [PuntoSicuro](https://www.puntosicuro.it/magazine/view-pdf/le-novita-per-la-formazione-degli-addetti-al-servizio-antincendio-AR-21678/); [Vigili del Fuoco info sheet](https://www.vigilfuoco.it/sites/default/files/2024-04/2023%20-%20DL%2081%20-%20informazioni%20per%20l%27utenza%20%284%29.pdf)
- **Sanctions** under D.Lgs. 81/2008, revalued +15.9% by DM/DD n. 111 of 20 Sept 2023 (GU n. 242 of 16 Oct 2023; INL nota 724 of 30 Oct 2023). Sources differ on the start date (1 July 2023 vs violations from 6 Oct 2023). The revaluation stacks on the +10% of L. 145/2018 — [INL nota 724/2023](https://www.ispettorato.gov.it/files/2023/10/inlavoro.INL-DCVIG.REGISTRO-UFFICIALEU.0000724.30-10-2023.pdf); [Ipsoa](https://www.ipsoa.it/documents/quotidiano/2023/10/25/salute-sicurezza-lavoro-aumentano-sanzioni-ammende-violazioni)
- **Missing risk assessment / DVR** (art. 29 c. 1, sanction in art. 55), non-high-risk firms: arrest of 3–6 months or a fine of €3,559.60–9,112.57. Extinction by compliance and payment under D.Lgs. 758/1994 cited at €2,278.14 — [pmi.it](https://www.pmi.it/?p=87848); [legislazionetecnica](https://www.legislazionetecnica.it/node/6400060)

### Inferences
- **Who in a pizzeria (unverified this session):**
  - The ristorazione sector (ATECO I) was "rischio basso" under the 2011 accords (4h specific training). Kitchens with ovens, knives and burns are often treated as medium by prudent RSPPs. Under the 2025 Accordo, the sector's class must be checked in its annex.
  - The fire-risk level of a small pizzeria is usually level 1 (refresh 2h) under DM 2/9/2021 unless the DVR says otherwise; check this.
- **Primo soccorso and the medico competente (unverified this session):**
  - Primo soccorso (DM 388/2003): a pizzeria with fewer than 3 workers is Group C, with 3 or more Group B. The course is 12h, with a refresh every 3 years (4h practical).
  - Medico competente and sorveglianza sanitaria (art. 41 D.Lgs. 81/2008): only when the DVR finds a risk that requires it (e.g. manual handling of loads, night work, microclimate near ovens). Many pizzerias appoint one for night work or manual handling.
- **The DVR** must be revised after significant changes to the work process, after serious injuries, or when health surveillance shows it is needed (art. 29 c. 3, from general knowledge). It is not on a fixed calendar.
- **Deadline for Gennaro:** under the secondary sources' "24 months for employer courses", Gennaro's 16h datore di lavoro course would be due by about 24 May 2027 if he has no recognised earlier training. Verify the exact transitional rule and any exemptions for existing DL-RSPPs in the GU text.
- **Where the record lives:** none of these trainings or the DVR is in any official online register. Certificates and the DVR live at the business (paper/PDF) and with the training provider. Inspectors (ASL Napoli 1 SPSAL, INL, NAS for food) check on site, so nothing is machine-readable from an official source.

## 5. Food: Campania alimentarista training, HACCP, controls

### Takeaway
In Campania the old libretto sanitario was replaced from 1 June 2006 by a training attestato (DD n. 46 of 23 Feb 2005; criteria DD n. 9 of 13 Mar 2006). Pizza makers are risk level 1 (4h initial course), cooks level 2 (8h), responsabili 12h, with a refresh every 3 years (3h, or 6h for responsabili). The 2018 regional decrees (DD 76/2018, DD 110/2018) were partly struck down by the Consiglio di Stato in 2020, so the current consolidated rule needs checking with ASL Napoli 1. The owner (OSA) is legally responsible for staff training under Reg. CE 852/2004.

### Cited Findings
- DD n. 46/2005 replaced the LISA (libretto di idoneità sanitaria) with a training attestato from 1 June 2006, model in its annex B — [Campania DD 46/2005, via EpiCentro ISS](https://www.epicentro.iss.it/ebp/docs/campania%20DD%20n46%202005.pdf); [Allegato A](https://www.epicentro.iss.it/ebp/pdf/Campania_decreto46_2005-Allegato_A.pdf)
- **Risk levels:** level 1 includes "addetti alla produzione di pizze e similari", baristi and fornai; level 2 includes cuochi and pasticcieri. Initial hours: level 1 4h, level 2 8h, responsabili di industrie alimentari 12h. Refresh every 3 years (or sooner with new production or technology): 3h for levels 1–2, 6h for responsabili. The ASL and accredited public or private bodies deliver the courses (DD 9/2006). Article dated 2017 — [PuntoSicuro, 12 Jun 2017](https://www.puntosicuro.it/sicurezza-alimentare-haccp-C-91/igiene-alimentare-la-formazione-haccp-in-campania-umbria-AR-17016/)
- The 2018 decree annexes repeat the 4/8/12h tables and the 3-year refresh. They place legal responsibility for missing initial or refresher training on the food business operator (OSA) under Reg. 852/2004 — [Allegato 1 DD 110 of 24/5/2018](https://www.edafos.it/normativa/regione/campania/allegato-1-decreto-110-del-24-5-2018-requisiti-normativa-formazione-haccp-campania.pdf); [Allegato 1 DD 76 of 17/4/2018](https://edafos.it/normativa/regione/campania/allegato-1-decreto-76-del-17-04-2018-requisiti-normativa-formazione-haccp-campania.pdf)
- Consiglio di Stato judgment 7346/2020 declared DD 76/2018 illegitimate in part. It kept e-learning but required an in-person final exam, and it struck limits on recognising certificates from bodies accredited in other regions — [Rivista di Diritto Agrario, 2021](https://rivistadiagraria.org/articoli/anno-2021/formazione-degli-alimentaristi-consiglio-cassa-la-regione-campania/?print=pdf)
- One secondary source mentions a 5-year refresh, which conflicts with the 3-year rule in the regional acts — [search summary, sources above]
- **Sanctions:** I found no reliable official amount for missing food-handler training under D.Lgs. 27/2021 (as amended by L. 71/2021). A commercial blog gives €500–30,000 for HACCP violations generally (not authoritative) — [certifico on L. 71/2021](https://certifico.com/haccp/legislazione-haccp/legge-21-maggio-2021-n-71); [qamarero (non-official)](https://qamarero.com/it/blog/sanzioni-haccp-importi-controlli-e-come-proteggerti/)

### Inferences

## 6. Is there an official place listing an employer's own upcoming obligations?

### Takeaway
No single official calendar lists one employer's upcoming duties across INPS, INAIL, safety and food. The closest things are partial:
- **INPS Cassetto previdenziale artigiani e commercianti, "Dati del mod. F24":** the owner's own fixed installments, amounts and dates, plus an email alert when they're published. Web UI only.
- **INPS Cassetto previdenziale aziende:** the employer's debts and contribution position.
- **INAIL's autoliquidazione services:** premium and installments.
- **DURC online:** regularity status.

Safety training, the DVR and food-handler certificates have no official online record at all; their deadlines are tracked by the consulente, the RSPP or the training provider.

### Cited Findings
- The F24 data in the Cassetto A/C give the amounts due, deadlines and payment codes for the year's contributions, and INPS emails the holder or intermediary when they're ready — [INPS messaggio 1792/2020](https://servizi2.inps.it/MessaggiZIP/Messaggio%20numero%201792%20del%2029-04-2020.pdf); [pensionioggi](https://www.pensionioggi.it/notizie/lavoro/artigiani-e-commercianti-disponibili-i-modelli-f24-per-il-versamento-dei-contributi-876786)
- The Cassetto previdenziale del contribuente is the single access point to a company's INPS contribution position, for employers, legal representatives and delegated intermediaries — [INPS circolare 34/2023](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circolare-INPS-31-marzo-2023-n.-34.pdf)
- DURC online gives a regularity result by PEC, valid 120 days — [fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/scaricare-durc-online/)
- INPS's machine interfaces (133 PDND e-services) serve public administrations, not employers or third-party tools — [INPS news Jul 2025](https://www.inps.it/it/it/inps-comunica/notizie/dettaglio-news-page.news.2025.07.piattaforma-digitale-nazionale-dati-i-servizi-erogati-dall-inps.html)
