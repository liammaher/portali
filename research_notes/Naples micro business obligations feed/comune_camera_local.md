# Recurring LOCAL obligations of a Naples micro-business (pizzeria, ditta individuale) — Comune di Napoli, CCIAA Napoli, SIAE/SCF, RAI — and where "due"/"done" is recorded

Persona: Gennaro, pizzeria in Naples, ditta individuale, 3 employees, dehors on public land, insegna, background music, PEC, SPID. Research date 2026-10-27. Pages fetched on that date; "last updated" stamps quoted where the page shows one. Anything not confirmed on a primary source is marked.

## 1. TARI non domestica, Comune di Napoli, 2026

### Takeaway
TARI 2026 in Naples is self-assessed ("autoliquidazione") and paid in four instalments: three acconti (30 Sep, 30 Oct, 30 Nov 2026; or a single acconto by 15 Oct 2026) computed at 75% on the 2025 tariffs, then a saldo on 16 Feb 2027 on the 2026 tariffs (not yet adopted). The Comune sends the avviso by **ordinary post**; the official page doesn't mention PEC or app IO for TARI. Errors are reported by PEC. Coercive collection (riscossione coattiva) goes to Napoli Obiettivo Valore (a joint venture with Municipia).

### Cited Findings
- Regolamento TARI: Deliberazione C.C. n. 37 of 19 June 2023. The 2025 tariffs (basis of the 2026 acconti): Deliberazione C.C. n. 31 of 30 April 2025. The 2026 acconto was set by Deliberazione G.C. n. 210 of 15 May 2026. The 2026 tariffs (for the saldo) still await a future Council deliberation; the page gives no number or date — [Comune di Napoli, TARI 2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/tari-tassa-sui-rifiuti/tari-2026/) (last updated 02/10/2026)
- Instalments: 1st acconto by 30 Sep 2026, 2nd by 30 Oct 2026, 3rd by 30 Nov 2026; single acconto payment by 15 Oct 2026; the three acconti equal 75% of the amount due on 2025 tariffs; saldo 16 Feb 2027 on 2026 tariffs — [Comune di Napoli, TARI 2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/tari-tassa-sui-rifiuti/tari-2026/); same dates in [NapoliToday](https://www.napolitoday.it/economia/tari-2026-scadenze-acconti-rate.html) and [Cityrumors, 25 Aug 2026](https://napoli.cityrumors.it/2026/08/25/napoli-tari-2026-scadenze-e-modalita-di-pagamento/). Conflict: [Sky TG24, 26 Jun 2026](https://tg24.sky.it/economia/2026/06/26/tari-2026-scadenza) gives 16 Dec 2026 for the saldo. The Comune's page (16 Feb 2027) is authoritative.
- "Il versamento della TARI è in autoliquidazione": the business must pay on the dates even if no avviso arrives — [Comune di Napoli, TARI 2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/tari-tassa-sui-rifiuti/tari-2026/)
- The Ufficio sends the payment notices "tramite posta ordinaria" before the deadlines. If the notice doesn't match your situation, compute the tax yourself and send the changes by PEC to entrate.tari-tares-tarsu@pec.comune.napoli.it. The page doesn't mention app IO or pagoPA for TARI — [Comune di Napoli, TARI 2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/tari-tassa-sui-rifiuti/tari-2026/)
- Online tool: a "simulatore di calcolo" at https://portaletributi.comune.napoli.it/?ente=NAPO computes the tax and prints F24 forms. On the TARI 2026 page, the "Accedi all'area personale" link points to "#" (it's empty), and SPID is not mentioned — [Comune di Napoli, TARI 2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/tari-tassa-sui-rifiuti/tari-2026/)
- Perequative components charged per utenza in 2026: UR1 €0.10, UR2 €1.50, UR3 €6.00. The bonus sociale rifiuti (25% off for low ISEE) is for domestic users and is applied in 2027 — [Comune di Napoli, TARI 2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/tari-tassa-sui-rifiuti/tari-2026/)
- Contacts: PEC entrate.tari-tares-tarsu@pec.comune.napoli.it; URP PEC urp@pec.comune.napoli.it; centralino 081 7951111; "Richiedi assistenza" and "Prenota appuntamento" on portalecittadino.comune.napoli.it. The page notes a temporary closure of the Sportelli Informativi Tributari at via Diocleziano 330, without dates — [Comune di Napoli, TARI 2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/tari-tassa-sui-rifiuti/tari-2026/)
- How the amount is computed: for non-domestic users the fixed part (quota fissa) is the per-m² tariff applied to the taxable area; the variable part (quota variabile) relates to the waste produced — [Regolamento TARI (PDF)](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Regolamento_IUC_TARI.pdf?mode=download), as summarised in search results
- An illustrative example on the national TARI transparency portal for Napoli: a 100 m² bar/café, tariffa fissa €2.58/m² and tariffa variabile €3.79/m². It's an example, not a confirmed pizzeria rate — [Portale Trasparenza TARI, Napoli](https://www.trasparenzatari.it/trasparenzatari/index.php?COMUNE=F839)
- 2025 tariffs: non-domestic users saw "variazioni minime (+0,3%)" — [NapoliToday, Tari 2025 tariffs](https://www.napolitoday.it/attualita/tari-napoli-tariffe-2025.html)
- Riscossione coattiva: the Comune entrusted it to Napoli Obiettivo Valore (NOV), the special-purpose company set up with Municipia. In July 2026 NOV's president said €304m+ had been recovered over three years; TARI evasion recovery reached 31.4%, with €43m+ collected (the company's own figures) — [Fanpage](https://www.fanpage.it/napoli/napoli-riscossione-tasse-privati), [Quotidiano.net](https://www.quotidiano.net/napoli/tasse-evasione-fiscale-ff06f682)
- Corte costituzionale sentenza n. 62 of 30 April 2026 upheld the rule that project companies like NOV need not register separately in the register of private collectors. Taxpayers had challenged IMU/TARI notices issued by NOV on that ground — [QuotidianoPiù](https://www.quotidianopiu.it/dettaglio/15259408/riscossione-locale-legittimita-delle-societa-di-progetto-senza-albo)

### Inferences
- Tariff category: under DPR 158/1999 (comuni over 5,000 inhabitants), a pizzeria falls in category 22 "Ristoranti, trattorie, osterie, pizzerie, pub". This comes from the national method; I didn't verify it on Naples' tariff annex. The bill is roughly mq × (Tf_cat22 + Tv_cat22) plus the UR components and the TEFA provincial surcharge (TEFA not confirmed on the Naples page).
- An accertamento is in practice an "avviso di accertamento esecutivo" (L. 160/2019, art. 1 c. 792). It's usually notified by PEC to a business's domicilio digitale, or by registered post/SEND. The general rules give 60 days to pay or appeal to the Corte di giustizia tributaria di primo grado di Napoli; autotutela (a request to the Ufficio TARI by PEC) doesn't suspend the 60 days. These are general national rules; I found no Naples page stating them.
- "Due" lives on: the paper avviso; the portaletributi simulator (amount only). "Done" lives on: the F24 receipt; Agenzia Entrate's cassetto fiscale (F24 versamenti, codice 3944). No API exists for the Comune's TARI position. The record of what's due is effectively **paper**, plus a logged-in web portal of uncertain scope.

- Whether Naples sends TARI avvisi to app IO (no source found; the official page says post only).
- Naples-specific deadlines for accertamenti and the ricorso route (no official page found).

## 2. Canone unico patrimoniale (CUP): dehors (occupazione suolo) and insegna (esposizioni pubblicitarie)

### Takeaway
Both CUP components are annual and self-assessed, and since 2026 each is paid on a pagoPA avviso the Comune sends before the first deadline. For 2026 the occupazione-di-suolo dates were moved (Delib. 195 of 30 Apr 2026) to **31 Aug, 30 Oct, 30 Dec 2026**. The pubblicità page still shows the ordinary 30 Apr / 30 Jul / 30 Oct dates (instalments above €350). Permanent occupations are payable only via pagoPA on **Partenopay** from 1 Mar 2026. The national relaxed dehors regime runs to **30 Jun 2027** (L. 182/2025 art. 50), not 31 Dec 2027.

### Cited Findings
- Deliberazione n. 195/2026 of 30 April 2026 postponed the 2026 CUP/COSAP dates: rata unica or 1st rata by 31 Aug 2026, 2nd rata by 30 Oct 2026, 3rd rata by 30 Dec 2026 — [Comune di Napoli, Occupazione di suolo](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/occupazione-di-suolo/) (last updated 14/10/2026)
- From 2026, payment is by a pagoPA avviso that the entrate service sends before the first due date. The channel (post/PEC/IO) isn't stated. If you self-assessed and then got an updated avviso, you pay the difference to postal accounts 49542608 (permanent concessions already granted) or 49543655 (temporary and new permanent) — [Comune di Napoli, Occupazione di suolo](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/occupazione-di-suolo/)
- The CUP is in autoliquidazione: not receiving the avviso bonario doesn't excuse late payment — [Comune di Napoli, Canone Unico Patrimoniale](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/)
- From 1 March 2026, permanent occupations can be paid only via pagoPA on the "Partenopay" platform — [Comune di Napoli, Occupazione suolo pubblico permanente](https://www.comune.napoli.it/servizio/occupazione-di-suolo-pubblico-permanente/). Partenopay pays pagoPA notices (school meals, nursery, fines, local taxes) with or without login, using the notice code and codice fiscale — [Partenopay](https://partenopay.comune.napoli.it/)
- Formula for occupazione (Regolamento art. 39): C = T × D × S × α. T = tariff by road category A/B/C (Stradario, Allegato F); D = duration (permanent: start and end months counted in full; temporary: per day); S = m², fractions rounded up; α = coefficient from Allegato B (1 if the type isn't listed). 2026 permanent tariffs, €/m² per year: A 186.545, B 143.207, C 84.742. Temporary, €/m² per day: A 13.712, B 9.592, C 6.844. Tariffs are indexed at 75% of FOI (art. 38) — [Comune di Napoli, Occupazione di suolo](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/occupazione-di-suolo/)
- Regolamento CUP: Delib. C.C. n. 16 of 30 May 2022 (L. 160/2019 art. 1 cc. 816–836). Instalment plans: Delib. C.C. n. 135 of 14 Oct 2025, up to 84 monthly instalments of at least €50 each, lost after 3 missed instalments, unavailable once coercive recovery has started; request by PEC to entrate.cosap@pec.comune.napoli.it — [Comune di Napoli, Occupazione di suolo](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/occupazione-di-suolo/)
- Who does what: concessions for spaces in front of somministrazione businesses (dehors) are handled by the **SUAP**, temporary or permanent; most other permanent occupations by the Municipalità. Billing and the avviso by the Servizio responsabile dell'entrata. Unpaid canone leads to an avviso di accertamento esecutivo (art. 50) with a penalty and interest at the legal rate plus 1.5 points. An unauthorised occupation pays an indemnity of canone +50%, plus a penalty of twice the indemnity (25% less if paid within 60 days). Ravvedimento operoso is possible before notification (not for occupations without a concession). Office: Ufficio CUP Occupazione di Suolo, via Diocleziano 330, Mon–Fri 8:30–12:30, PEC entrate.cosap@pec.comune.napoli.it — [Comune di Napoli, Occupazione di suolo](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/occupazione-di-suolo/)
- Insegna/pubblicità formula: T = Tmax × a × b × c × d per m². Tmax is €145/m² per year (permanent). a = zone (red 1, green 0.90, light blue 0.85, yellow 0.80); b = 1 if the street is on Allegato G, else 0.9; c = size (≤5.50 m² 0.75; 5.51–8.50 m² 0.90; >8.51 m² 1); d = type (luminous 1, non-luminous 0.75, **insegna di esercizio 0.70**). 2023 tariffs (G.C. 186 of 08/06/2023) still in force. Permanent authorisations self-assessed by 30 April; above €350, instalments 30 Apr / 30 Jul / 30 Oct ("dates may change, check the portal"). Paid by a pagoPA avviso sent before the first deadline. From 01/01/2026 Napoli Servizi S.p.A. no longer handles collection or accertamento. Office: Ufficio CUP Pubblicità, via Diocleziano 330; PEC canonepubblicita@pec.comune.napoli.it — [Comune di Napoli, Esposizioni pubblicitarie](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/esposizioni-pubblicitarie/) (last updated 14/10/2026)
- Search summary of the same pages: the pubblicità page still shows the ordinary dates and doesn't reflect the Delib. 195 deferral; check the avviso — [Comune di Napoli, CUP](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/)
- National dehors regime: L. 2 Dec 2025 n. 182 (Legge Semplificazioni; GU n. 281 of 3 Dec 2025; in force 18 Dec 2025), art. 50, extends the relaxed Covid-era dehors concessions (granted during the emergency and later renewed) to **30 June 2027**. Holders of ordinary concessions follow the normal renewal route. The deadline for the legislative decree reorganising dehors moved to 31 Dec 2026 — [PMI.it](https://www.pmi.it/impresa/normativa/483521/legge-semplificazioni-guida-alle-novita-per-imprese-e-professionisti.html), [Gambero Rosso](https://www.gamberorosso.it/notizie/attualita/dehors-liberi-fino-meta-2027/), [Altalex](https://www.altalex.com/documents/2025/12/10/legge-semplificazioni-2025-nuove-misure-ambito-urbanistico-edilizio). BibLus writes "30 giugno 2025", an apparent typo — [BibLus](https://biblus.acca.it/legge-semplificazioni-2025/). GU text not verified directly.

### Inferences
- Worked example (illustrative, using the official formula): an 8 m² permanent dehors on a category-B street with α = 1 costs 143.207 × 8 = €1,145.66/year, before any coefficient below 1 that Allegato B might give dehors (I didn't see a dehors coefficient; the 0.75 one is for awnings). For a 1.5 m² non-luminous insegna di esercizio in the yellow zone, off-list street: 145 × 0.80 × 0.9 × 0.75 × 0.70 × 1.5 ≈ €82. The regolamento's exemptions (arts. 46–47; nationally, insegne di esercizio up to 5 m² are exempt under L. 160/2019 c. 833) may make it zero. The 5 m² exemption is national law, not confirmed on the Naples page.
- Both components are annual, per concession or authorisation. The dehors concession itself (SUAP) has its own expiry: for Covid-regime concessions, 30 Jun 2027. The concession expiry is on the concession act (paper or PDF at SUAP), not in a tax portal.
- "Due": the pagoPA avviso, which can be paid on Partenopay by notice code. "Done": the pagoPA receipt (ricevuta telematica). A pagoPA notice the Comune emits **may** appear in app IO's payments section if the Comune sends a message, but that's unconfirmed for the CUP.

## 3. Camera di Commercio di Napoli: diritto annuale 2026, PEC/domicilio digitale, impresa.italia.it

### Takeaway
For a ditta individuale in the sezione speciale (piccolo imprenditore/artigiano), the 2026 diritto annuale in Naples is €44 for the sede (+€9 per unità locale). In the sezione ordinaria it's €100 (+€20 per UL). The **CCIAA Napoli doesn't apply the 20% increase** from 2026 (Delib. consiliare n. 10 of 30/10/2025). Firms registered at 1 Jan 2026 get a letter by PEC around the first income-tax acconto deadline. The firm's paid/unpaid status is visible with SPID in the Cassetto digitale (impresa.italia.it).

### Cited Findings
- CCIAA Napoli doesn't use the art. 18 c. 10 L. 580/1993 increase from 2026 (Delib. consiliare n. 10 of 30/10/2025). 2026 amounts: imprese individuali sezione speciale €44 sede / €9 UL; sezione ordinaria (individual firms included) €100 sede / €20 UL. With ULs, add the unrounded figures and round only the total (e.g. sede + 1 UL = €52.80 → €53) — [CCIAA Napoli, Diritto annuale 2026](https://www.na.camcom.gov.it/registro-imprese-e-albi/diritto-annuale/diritto-annuale-2026)
- Firms registered at 1 Jan 2026 receive a notice with payment instructions, usually around the first income-tax acconto deadline, sent to the firm's **PEC**. Arrears from earlier years appear on the letter's last page. Firms without a valid PEC must restore or replace it promptly and tell the Registro Imprese. New registrations pay at filing or within 30 days by F24, or by Telemaco prepaid. Office: diritto.annuale@na.camcom.it — [CCIAA Napoli, Diritto annuale 2026](https://www.na.camcom.gov.it/registro-imprese-e-albi/diritto-annuale/diritto-annuale-2026)
- Ordinary deadline for firms registered at 1 Jan 2026: 30 June 2026, or by 30 July with +0.40% — [Fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/diritto-camerale/), [Fisco Imprese](https://fiscoimprese.it/diritto-annuale-camera-di-commercio-2026/) (secondary sources)
- Conflict: secondary sources mention a DM of 17 March 2026 authorising 20% increases, with a conguaglio by 30 Nov for those who already paid — [FiscoeTasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/3405-diritto-camerale-2026-il%20mimit-pubblica-le-maggiorazioni-del-20.html). This applies only to chambers that asked for it; Naples' own page says it doesn't apply the increase — [CCIAA Napoli](https://www.na.camcom.gov.it/registro-imprese-e-albi/diritto-annuale/diritto-annuale-2026). Other sources say the national measures were unchanged for 2026 — [Edotto](https://www.edotto.com/articolo/camere-di-commercio-misure-del-diritto-annuale-2026-invariate)
- Cassetto digitale dell'imprenditore (impresa.italia.it): free; the legal representative or titolare logs in with SPID/CNS/CIE (web and app). It shows visura, atti, pratiche sent, the latest bilanci; follows the status of SUAP pratiche and opens their fascicolo informatico d'impresa where the Chamber manages the SUAP; shows the **diritto annuale payment status** (amount, year) and can pay it from the app; e-invoices — [Confindustria blog](https://kosakiama.confindustria.it/blogs/83d41c5d-f5f2-412f-a16c-450c56b70284/entry/cassetto_digitale_dell_imprenditore_alcune_informazioni?lang=en_us), [Namirial](https://focus.namirial.com/it/cassetto-digitale-dellimprenditore/), [CCIAA Trento press release 2021](https://www.tn.camcom.it/sites/default/files/uploads/documents/ComunicatiStampa/Anno_2021/21CSCassettoDigitaleDell%27Imprenditore.pdf) (sources dated 2017–2024; current features not checked live)

### Inferences
- Payment is by F24 with codice tributo 3850 (diritto annuale), ente code "NA", year 2026, in the same F24 as the first IRPEF acconto. National practice; not stated on the Naples page fetched.
- For a pizzeria titolare (not an artisan), the sezione depends on whether he's registered as a piccolo imprenditore (speciale, €44) or ordinaria (€100); the visura says which.
- PEC/domicilio digitale: a ditta individuale must keep a valid, active PEC registered at the Registro Imprese (DL 76/2020 strengthened this, with a sanction and an ex officio domicilio if it's missing). This is national law and not fetched here. An expired PEC also means the CCIAA letter and any PEC-notified accertamento never arrive.
- "Due/done" for the diritto annuale is the one item in this set with a **logged-in web/app view** of paid/unpaid (Cassetto digitale). Programmatic access exists only for intermediaries via InfoCamere/Telemaco (paid services); I found no public owner API.

### Gaps
- The exact 2026 deadline dates on the CCIAA Napoli page (it didn't state 30 June/30 July), the codice tributo and the ente code (from general knowledge, not confirmed this session).
- Whether impresa.italia.it currently exposes an API or export for the diritto annuale status.

## 4. Music and TV: SIAE, SCF, RAI canone speciale

### Takeaway
Since 1 Jan 2026 SIAE collects, together with its own licence, the related-rights fees of SCF and Nuovo IMAIE for background music in shops and public venues. The 2026 SIAE renewal was moved from 28 Feb to **10 Mar 2026**; the SCF fee was due by **31 May 2026**. The RAI canone speciale for a TV in a public venue is due by 31 Jan (2 Feb in 2026), or in quarters (30 Apr, 31 Jul, 31 Oct). For esercizi pubblici of 1st/2nd category it's €1,018.40/year.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 Jan 2026, SIAE, SCF and Nuovo IMAIE collect together for background music, shops included — [SCF press release](https://www.scfitalia.it/comunicati-stampa/siae-scf-nuovo-imaie-2026-insieme-negozi.kl); [MyCorporateRadio](https://www.mycorporateradio.com/newsroom/news/diritto-autore-2026-riscossione-unificata-siae-scf/)
- 2026 SIAE background-music renewal: ordinary deadline 28 Feb 2026, moved by SIAE's Direzione Generale to 10 Mar 2026. Tariffs unchanged; association discounts around 15–20%, up to 27% — [Confcommercio Ascom Bologna, 28 Jan 2026](https://ascom.bo.it/2026/01/28/rinnovo-siae-scf-2026/), [Confcommercio Lazio Nord](https://www.confcommerciolazionord.it/siae-2026/). Some sources gave 2 Mar 2026 for the discount — [Confesercenti Prato](https://prato.confesercenti.it/2026/02/02/siae/)
- SCF payment deadline 31 May 2026 for public venues and shops; 15% discount for association members — [Confesercenti Milano](https://milano.confesercenti.it/musica-dambiente-2026-convenzione-confesercenti-siae-e-scf/), [Confartigianato Firenze](https://www.confartigianatofirenze.it/convenzioni-siae-e-scf-2026-per-le-imprese-artigiane/)
- RAI canone speciale 2026 (2025 tariffs confirmed): esercizi pubblici of 1st and 2nd category €1,018.40 annual / €519.58 half-year / €270.18 quarterly; 3rd and 4th category €407.35 / €207.82 / €108.07; luxury €2,036.83. Radio only: €29.94/year. Due 31 Jan 2026 (Saturday, so 2 Feb accepted); quarterly payments by 30 Apr, 31 Jul, 31 Oct. Paid by card (800.938.362), c/c postale 2105, direct debit (annual only), or transfer to a personal virtual IBAN; F24 isn't mentioned — [InformazioneFiscale](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/partite-iva-scadenza-canone-rai-speciale-2026-importi-tariffe); [InvestireOggi](https://www.investireoggi.it/canone-rai-speciale-2026-chi-paga-e-come-entro-il-2-febbraio/)

### Inferences
- These aren't public bodies' records. The "due" signal is a SIAE renewal notice (to SIAE customers; channel not confirmed) or a trade association's reminder. The record lives in the SIAE customer area (portale SIAE, login) and in RAI's abbonamenti speciali records; there's no public API and no app IO integration. Who does it: normally the owner, or the trade association (Confcommercio/Confesercenti/FIPE), which also applies the discount.
- A pizzeria is most likely an esercizio pubblico of 2nd/3rd category for the RAI canone; which category applies wasn't confirmed.

### Gaps
- SIAE/SCF tariff amounts for a small pizzeria (by m² or seats); not found.
- How SIAE notifies renewals (PEC, email, post) and whether its customer portal (SPID?) shows paid/unpaid.

## 5. Other local recurring obligations (ordinances, VVF, prices) and app IO / notifications

### Takeaway
Naples' 2026 movida ordinances were zone-specific and temporary: piazza Bellini, 6 Mar – 6 May 2026, with no takeaway sales from 22:30 to 06:00, closing by 00:30 Sun–Thu and 01:30 Fri–Sat, fines €2,000–20,000. I found none in force in October 2026. The Comune di Napoli has 42 services on app IO, with pagoPA notices and payment reminders, and has joined **SEND** for legally valid digital notices. No source confirms that TARI or CUP notices go to IO.

### Cited Findings
- Piazza Bellini ordinance (Mayor Manfredi), 6 Mar – 6 May 2026: no takeaway sales or service of alcoholic and soft drinks from 22:30 to 06:00 (another article says from 22:00); closing by 00:30 Sun–Thu and 01:30 Fri–Sat, plus 30 minutes for cleaning; fines €2,000–20,000 — [Fanpage](https://www.fanpage.it/napoli/napoli-stretta-sulla-movida-a-piazza-bellini-nuovi-orari-e-stop-allasporto-notturno-per-due-mesi/), [Napolike](https://www.napolike.it/movida-napoli-stretta-su-piazza-bellini-nuovi-orari-e-multe-fino-a-20mila-euro)

### Inferences
- If the Comune notifies a TARI or CUP accertamento through SEND, a business with a PEC on INI-PEC gets it by PEC (legally valid on delivery), plus an IO alert if Gennaro has IO with SPID. That would make PEC and IO the most reliable signal of what's "due" for accertamenti, while ordinary avvisi still arrive by post.
- Vigili del Fuoco: a pizzeria with a wood oven and typical seating usually falls below the DPR 151/2011 thresholds (e.g. >100 people for public venues, activity 65), so there's no periodic SCIA antincendio renewal. That's national law and depends on the venue; not researched here.
- Price display (menu/prices visible outside and inside) is a regional/national rule with no recurring filing; nothing to record.
