# Compute what Gennaro owes, then collect proof it's paid

The "what you owe this month" half of Gennaro's feed can be built. The "and whether it's done" half can be built only partly today. Every recurring obligation of a Naples pizzeria run as a ditta individuale with three employees can be dated from public rules plus facts the vault already holds: about 25 money and filing deadlines a year, plus per-person renewal cycles. A few amounts can also be priced up front: INPS fixed contributions of **€4,611.64 a year in four F24 instalments of €1,152.91**, a Camera di Commercio fee of **€44 or €100**, and the CUP (canone unico) for the dehors and insegna from the Comune's published formula. The rest (IVA, ritenute, IRPEF, INAIL, payroll INPS) only exists as figures in the commercialista's and consulente's working papers until their F24s arrive. The official records of what is due and what is done are each held by one ente: the Agenzia delle Entrate's cassetto fiscale, two INPS cassetti, INAIL's portal, pagoPA receipts, the Chamber's impresa italia. Each sits behind SPID in a web interface, or behind an intermediary's delega. None offers a read API to the owner or to a third-party tool, apart from one bulk-CU API that is open only to delegated intermediaries since July 2026. Training, HACCP and DVR records have no official online register at all. So the tool can legitimately compute the calendar, read Gennaro's PEC and receipt emails, parse the F24s and pagoPA notices that reach him, and track certificates he stores. It cannot hold his SPID or read his IO inbox, and it cannot act as an EquiPro or PDND party. "Done" therefore has to come from evidence that reaches him plus one periodic look at the cassetto fiscale, which he makes himself. The fact that holds the design together: **every F24 lands in the cassetto fiscale's Versamenti section whoever paid it.** One look there confirms most of the money, which makes the feed tractable.

## About 25 dated deadlines, two studios and a postman make up Gennaro's year

Gennaro's obligations split by who handles them, not by ente, and that split decides how he hears of each one. The commercialista prepares the income-tax and IVA side. With three employees, the forfettario is effectively ruled out: its 2026 cap on staff costs is **€20,000 gross** ([fisco7](https://www.fisco7.it/2026/01/legge-di-bilancio-forfettari-le-novita-del-2026/)). He is therefore on ordinary rules, with IRPEF acconti and periodic IVA. As a persona fisica he is outside IRAP since 2022, employees or not ([NT+ Fisco](https://ntplusfisco.ilsole24ore.com/art/esenti-dall-irap-anche-imprese-familiari-AEPsTxAB)). IVA is quarterly if his prior-year volume stays under **€500,000** (somministrazione counts as a service; that classification is an inference) and monthly otherwise. Quarterly payments carry 1% interest ([money.it](https://www.money.it/liquidazione-iva-come-funziona-come-si-fa-versamento)). The consulente del lavoro runs the monthly payroll cycle: an F24 on the 16th for IRPEF ritenute and INPS employee contributions, which the employer owes even when wages are late ([INPS](https://inps.it/it/it/inps-comunica/diritti-e-obblighi-in-materia-di-sicurezza-sociale-nell-unione-e/per-le-imprese/pagamento-dei-contributi-previdenziali.html)). The same studio sends the UniEmens flow by the last day of the following month ([money.it](https://www.money.it/come-si-invia-una-denuncia-uniemens-all-inps)), and a UniLav by midnight the day before any hire or within five days of a termination, at a fine of **€100–500 per worker** if late ([Skello](https://www.skello.io/it/blog/modello-unilav-cose-e-come-funziona-la-comunicazione-obbligatoria-di-assunzione); [money.it](https://www.money.it/assunzione-agevolate-e-comunicazione-unilav-errori-da-evitare)).

Gennaro carries the local levies himself, and they are the riskiest because they are self-assessed. Naples TARI 2026 is "in autoliquidazione". The Comune posts the avviso by **ordinary mail**, but he must pay on the dates whether or not it arrives: acconti on 30 Sep, 30 Oct and 30 Nov 2026 at 75% of the 2025 tariffs, and a saldo on **16 Feb 2027** on 2026 tariffs that the Council has not yet adopted ([Comune di Napoli, TARI 2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/tari-tassa-sui-rifiuti/tari-2026/)). The CUP for the dehors is also self-assessed. From 2026 it is billed by a pagoPA avviso and paid on Partenopay, and Delibera 195/2026 moved its 2026 dates to **31 Aug, 30 Oct and 30 Dec** ([Comune di Napoli, occupazione di suolo](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/occupazione-di-suolo/)). The formula is public: tariff by road category × m² × coefficient. An 8 m² permanent dehors on a category-B street works out to about **€1,145.66 a year** (illustrative, at €143.207/m²). The insegna's CUP page still shows the ordinary 30 Apr / 30 Jul / 30 Oct instalments for bills above €350. A small non-luminous insegna di esercizio prices at about €82, and may be zero under the national 5 m² exemption, which was not confirmed on the Naples page ([Comune di Napoli, esposizioni pubblicitarie](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/esposizioni-pubblicitarie/)). The relaxed post-Covid dehors regime runs to **30 June 2027** ([PMI.it](https://www.pmi.it/impresa/normativa/483521/legge-semplificazioni-guida-alle-novita-per-imprese-e-professionisti.html)). That expiry is on his concession act, not in any tax portal.

Other bodies and private collectors fill out the year. The Chamber's diritto annuale is **€44** in the sezione speciale or **€100** in the ordinaria; Naples declined the 20% surcharge. It arrives as a letter to his PEC around the first IRPEF acconto ([CCIAA Napoli](https://www.na.camcom.gov.it/registro-imprese-e-albi/diritto-annuale/diritto-annuale-2026)). INPS Gestione Commercianti sets his own contributions: the fixed €4,611.64 on the €18,808 minimale, due 18 May, 20 Aug and 16 Nov 2026 and 16 Feb 2027, plus 24.48% on income above the minimale, paid with the IRPEF dates ([ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/lavoro/imposte-contributi-e-premi/contributi-e-premi/artigiani-commercianti-valori-contributivi-anno-2026/)). The INAIL premium is paid by 16 Feb or in four 25% instalments, with interest from the second; the last 2026 instalment is due **16 Nov** ([fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/3351-autoliquidazione-inail-20252026-scadenze-riduzioni-e-istruzioni-operative.html)). Since 1 Jan 2026 SIAE collects the background-music licence together with the SCF and Nuovo IMAIE fees. The 2026 renewal moved from 28 Feb to 10 Mar ([SCF](https://www.scfitalia.it/comunicati-stampa/siae-scf-nuovo-imaie-2026-insieme-negozi.kl); [Ascom Bologna](https://ascom.bo.it/2026/01/28/rinnovo-siae-scf-2026/)), and SCF's own deadline was 31 May ([Confesercenti Milano](https://milano.confesercenti.it/musica-dambiente-2026-convenzione-confesercenti-siae-e-scf/)). A TV in the dining room brings the RAI canone speciale: **€1,018.40** a year for 1st/2nd-category esercizi or €407.35 for 3rd/4th, due 31 Jan or in quarters ([InformazioneFiscale](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/partite-iva-scadenza-canone-rai-speciale-2026-importi-tariffe)). New in 2026, every POS must be linked to the registratore telematico in Fatture e Corrispettivi. Any new or changed POS has to be linked by the end of the second month after it changes, under a **€1,000–4,000** fine plus possible licence suspension ([fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1189-collegamento-cassa-e-pos-istruzioni-per-provvedere-dal-5-marzo.html)).

## Every record of "due" is private to its ente, and most notices are only a courtesy

No official body keeps a personal calendar for Gennaro's partita IVA. The Agenzia delle Entrate publishes a general scadenzario. Its personal messages, on app IO and by SMS or email, cover things like refunds, delegations and filing reminders, and no source shows them covering a business's periodic F24 ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/economia/2026/02/17/fisco-agenzia-entrate-app-io-notifiche-novita-2026)). Where a "due" record exists, it belongs to one ente and usually only after the fact. The INPS Cassetto previdenziale for artigiani and commercianti is the one true forward-looking record: it publishes the year's "Dati del mod. F24" with amounts, dates and codes, and emails the holder or intermediary when they are ready ([INPS messaggio 1792/2020](https://servizi2.inps.it/MessaggiZIP/Messaggio%20numero%201792%20del%2029-04-2020.pdf)). Everything else is a notice. The TARI avviso comes by post, the CUP is a pagoPA avviso, and the Chamber writes by PEC. AdE's avvisi bonari and compliance letters land in "L'Agenzia scrive" in the cassetto, which in July 2026 was widened to more liquidation and assessment notices ([provvedimento 16 Jul 2026](https://www.redigo.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Provvedimento-Cassetto-fiscale_16-luglio-2026.pdf)). For the big-ticket items (IVA, ritenute, IRPEF acconti), the only "due" record before payment is the studio's own computation.

That makes the self-assessment rule the central risk, and the main reason a feed is worth building. TARI and CUP both say explicitly that a missing avviso does not excuse a late payment ([Comune di Napoli, CUP](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/tributi-locali/canone-unico-patrimoniale/)). PEC timing works the same way. A notice delivered to his PEC takes effect on delivery, read or not ([tributiconsulting](https://tributiconsulting.it/cartelle-esattoriali/notifica/pec)). A SEND notification is perfected seven days after PEC delivery, or 15 days after a failed-delivery notice if his mailbox is full or invalid ([notifichedigitali.it](https://notifichedigitali.it/perfezionamento)). An avviso bonario then gives **60 days** to pay at one third of the penalty, or **90 days** when it went to his intermediary through Entratel ([ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/?p=12622)). A DURC found irregular gives at most 15 days to put things right ([fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/scaricare-durc-online/)). For a man who rarely opens his PEC, those clocks run unseen.

## The F24 is the spine: one cassetto sees every payment, whoever made it

The decisive structural fact is that the cassetto fiscale's Versamenti section shows every F24 paid in the taxpayer's name, whatever the channel. That includes F24s the commercialista sends through Entratel with a debit to Gennaro's IBAN, or with a single debit on the studio's own account ([AdE guide](https://assistenza.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/guida-consultazioni-cassetto-fiscale); [FiscoeTasse, addebito unico](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/normativa-prassi/10020-pagamenti-f24-da-parte-degli-intermediari-con-addebito-unico.html)). F24 covers IVA, IRPEF, ritenute, both INPS streams and INAIL, and probably also TARI and the diritto annuale. Both have F24 routes: the TARI simulator prints F24s, and the Chamber's fee is an F24 line. The codici tributo 3944 and 3850 are general knowledge, not confirmed in the notes. In practice, one page in one portal is the "done" record for nearly all of Gennaro's money. Qonto reports that receipts appear there about **five days after payment** ([PagamentiDigitali](https://www.pagamentidigitali.it/news/il-servizio-i24-di-qonto-delega-il-pagamento-di-f24-al-commercialista/)). AdE documents no latency, and the guide mentions no CSV export.

This matters most for the complaint the notes call structurally plausible: not knowing what the commercialista did. When a studio pays by Entratel addebito, Gennaro may never see the F24 before the debit. A 2026 Cassazione case reported by Fiscozen found that when a delegated F24 bounced, the bank's duty was to warn the intermediary, not the owner ([Fiscozen news](https://www.fiscozen.it/news/f24-inviati-dagli-intermediari-quando-la-banca-non-e-responsabile-per-lo-scarto/)). A feed that reconciles expected F24s against the cassetto is the owner's only independent check. It catches the F24 that was rejected, never sent, or sent for the wrong period.

The API horizon is real but not here yet. Provvedimento 200918 of 6 July 2026 set up an AdE "catalogo dei servizi di interoperabilità" for bulk, structured access to cassetto data, acts and communications. It is open in principle to taxpayers as well as intermediaries enabled for Entratel or Fisconline. The first service, bulk CU download, went live on 22 July 2026, and only for delegated intermediaries ([fisco7](https://www.fisco7.it/2026/07/agenzia-delle-entrate-servizi-del-cassetto-fiscale-tramite-api/); [quotidianopiu](https://www.quotidianopiu.it/dettaglio/15891815/cassetto-fiscale-al-via-lo-scarico-massivo-dei-dati-arrivano-i-servizi-api)). An F24 Versamenti flow is not announced, nor is a taxpayer self-activating one with his own client secret. If both arrive, a local tool could read "done" through API credentials Gennaro issues to himself. That would not be shared SPID, and it would change the economics of the whole design. This is the single development to watch, and the design should leave a slot for it.

## Legitimate automation stops at the edge of the owner's own inbox

Sorted by the rules the notes establish, the work falls into three bands.

The calendar is fully automatable. Dates follow from published rules and Gennaro's profile: IVA periodicity, employees, a dehors concession, an insegna, a TV, background music, his CCIAA sezione, and his INAIL and TARI instalment choices. Fixed amounts and formula amounts can be computed. So can the legal clocks that start when something lands in his PEC: SEND's seven days, the avviso bonario's 60 or 90, the DURC's 15. Certificate renewals follow from stored issue dates. None of this touches a protected system.

The inbound evidence can be automated with his consent, on his own machine. Reading his PEC over IMAP with an app password he creates is ordinary mailbox access. Read-only IMAP (EXAMINE plus BODY.PEEK) leaves his read state untouched. Notably, reading the PEC message does not perfect a SEND notification early; only opening the documents on SEND or IO does ([notifichedigitali.it](https://notifichedigitali.it/perfezionamento)). The PEC is also the one feed that carries everything SEND delivers to a business with an INI-PEC address. It carries the Chamber's letter and the DURC result, and probably AdER cartelle and AdE notices to the business (an inference the notes flag). Other parseable sources reach him the same way: the pagoPA avvisi themselves, identified by ente CF plus the 18-digit codice avviso; PSP receipt emails; INPS's "F24 data ready" email; AdE's SMS/email alerts; AdER's "Se mi scordo" reminders ([money.it](https://www.money.it/rottamazione-4-esiste-se-mi-scordo)); and, above all, the F24 PDFs the studios send. Running the reader locally, rather than in the cloud, keeps the tool's operator from becoming a GDPR art. 28 processor for a mailbox full of third parties' data (the notes' inference).

The rest cannot be automated legitimately, and the reasons differ. SPID credentials are personal, and AgID-linked material describes sharing them as prohibited, with the holder liable for what the other party does ([Agenda Digitale](https://www.agendadigitale.eu/cittadinanza-digitale/identita-digitale/spid-per-uso-professionale-come-funziona-e-chi-puo-usarlo/)). A tool that stores his SPID and OTP and scrapes the cassetti headlessly is therefore out. IO has a sender API for enti only, and no recipient API or export. SEND's recipient B2B API is unpublished and is for persone giuridiche, which a ditta individuale is not for SEND purposes ([pn-delivery](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pagopa/pn-delivery/main/docs/openapi/pn-delivery-b2b-pg-external.yaml); [Agenda Digitale](https://www.agendadigitale.eu/cittadinanza-digitale/send-le-notifiche-digitali-della-pa-per-cittadini-e-imprese-perche-e-utile/)). EquiPro is reserved to qualified intermediaries. PDND has been open to any registered company since January 2026, but joining grants nothing: each erogatore must approve a stated purpose with a legal basis, and no e-service found exposes a consenting citizen's debts or notices ([interop.pagopa.it](https://www.interop.pagopa.it/news/apertura-privati)). The POS–RT link is a web action only Gennaro or a delegate can take. Paying and filing stay with him and his studios.

Between the second and third bands sits a defensible middle route: the attended read. Gennaro logs in to the cassetto fiscale or the INPS cassetto himself, in a browser the tool drives on his machine. He types SPID and approves the push, and the tool reads the Versamenti page in his live session, or he saves the page as a PDF for the tool. The notes judge this close to "the owner using a browser extension" and distinct from credential sharing. That is an inference, not settled law, and portal anti-bot controls could interfere. A monthly ten-minute ritual of this kind confirms nearly every F24 item, which is enough to make "done" real.

## How to build it on pratiche-napoli and pvault

### The library must turn prose conditions into rules a machine can evaluate

pratiche-napoli already has most of the vocabulary. Procedures carry `applies_to.events` with an `annual` value. `deadline.rules` carry a `compute` block of the form "a vault date field + N days". `payment.schedule` lists dated instalments, each with a verification status. The INAIL autoliquidazione file already marks its computed 2027 date `unverified`, which is exactly the honesty the feed needs. Coverage is the first gap. The library has the INAIL premium, the diritto annuale, the food-handler attestato, the TARI declarations, the dehors concession and the insegna. It does not have the monthly F24 (ritenute and IVA), the IVA acconto and LIPE, Redditi/ISA, the 770 and CU, UniEmens, UniLav, the INPS commercianti contributions (it has only the registration), the CUP payment itself, SIAE/SCF, the RAI canone speciale, the POS–RT link, the DURC, or the D.Lgs. 81/2008 training cycle.

The second gap matters more for a feed than for a guide. `applies_to.conditions` are prose claims, and `compute` only adds days. The feed's real rules have calendar shapes. "The 16th of the month after, moved to the next working day" covers ritenute and IVA. "The last day of the following month" covers UniEmens. "From the 6th to the last day of the second month after a POS change" covers the link. "Every three years from issue" covers the attestato. Conditions are predicates on vault fields: IVA monthly or quarterly, at least one person with ruolo dipendente, a dehors concession present, a TV on the premises. Without machine-readable recurrence, a working-day shift rule and conditions keyed to schema fields, an agent would have to re-read prose every month and guess. The proposal (a design choice, not something the notes test) is an obligation entry that declares its recurrence, its shift rule, its applicability predicates against `schema.json` field IDs, and its amount kind: fixed, formula with vault inputs, from the avviso, or from the studio's F24. It would also declare who prepares it, where the "due" and "done" records live and who can read them (the matrix above), and which inbound signals to expect, such as the PEC sender of the CCIAA letter or the DURC. All of this carries the library's claim-level sources and statuses. The library also needs a year-specific override layer, because the dates move. In 2026 alone, DL 89/2026 moved the summer IRPEF payments, Delibera 195/2026 moved the CUP, and SIAE deferred its renewal. Sources also conflict: one national outlet gave 16 Dec 2026 for the TARI saldo against the Comune's 16 Feb 2027 ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/economia/2026/06/26/tari-2026-scadenza)). The library's rule that the ente's own page wins is the right tiebreaker.

### The vault already has the ledger; it needs instances, evidence and matching

On the pvault side, the cheapest path reuses what exists. Each period's obligation becomes a pratica, for example the November F24 or the TARI 3rd acconto. Pratiche already carry a stato, a canale and a deadline that shows in `pvault expiring` until the stato closes, and the store dates each closing. So "what you owe this month and whether it's done" is close to `pvault expiring` over generated instances. Whether the fixed stato list can express expected → notice received → F24 prepared → paid (claimed) → confirmed in the cassetto, or needs new values, is an open schema question. The business profile needs fields it may lack: IVA periodicity, ISA/CPB status, INAIL and TARI instalment choices, CCIAA sezione, RAI category, SIAE association membership, POS and RT identifiers with activation dates, insegna m² and lighting, and the dehors concession's regime and expiry. Per-person training and attestati belong as documents with `data_scadenza` on each person with a ruolo. `pvault expiring` already reports those, which makes the off-register obligations the easiest part of the whole feed. The vault's embedded stradario came partly from the 2003 COSAP civico ranges. It might help derive the CUP road category, but only if the categories match Allegato F, which no one has checked.

Evidence ingestion extends the PEC reader. A classifier matches incoming PEC against the library's expected signals. It extracts F24 PDFs (codici tributo, period, amount, due date), pagoPA avvisi (ente CF and codice avviso, for dedupe) and SEND receipts, and stamps each with its delivery time so legal clocks start on the right day. A second read-only IMAP source for his ordinary mailbox would capture PSP receipts and INPS/AdE email alerts. Each extraction should arrive as a proposal linked to an obligation instance, the same way connector-found files are proposals until the user accepts them. That keeps agents in the suggest-only role the vault already enforces. Matching then closes the loop. An avviso is closed by a receipt for the same (ente, IUV) pair. A studio F24 is closed by its line in the cassetto Versamenti, imported from the attended read or a saved PDF, allowing about five days' latency. An expected item with no evidence by T–3 becomes the feed's alarm. The vault's security model already rules out the forbidden paths: credentials stored only at the `personal` tier, and never readable by service tokens. It also never pays.

### The studios are the cheapest source of amounts

The highest-leverage collaborator is not an API but the commercialista and consulente. They already compute every IVA, ritenute and acconto figure. Fatture in Cloud lets a commercialista upload F24s to a client's account with a reminder ([Fatture in Cloud](https://www.fattureincloud.it/commercialisti/)), and TeamSystem sells client portals. If the studios send their F24s to an address the tool reads, the feed gains exact amounts with no new integration. In return the studio gets a client who sees what is still missing, which addresses the "lo studio rincorre i clienti" problem vendors describe ([Optlyx](https://optlyx.com/guida-portale-clienti-studio-commercialista)). The feed should also carry the meta-obligations no one else tracks. Pre-December-2025 delegations lapse by 28 February 2027 and must be re-conferred under the delega unica. A full PEC mailbox still lets notifications perfect at 15 days. The PEC registered on INI-PEC must stay valid, or the Chamber's letter never arrives.

Two things the notes do not settle should be decided before building. The first is the delivery surface. A local CLI vault cannot push to a pizzaiolo's phone without some relay, and every hosted relay reopens the GDPR processor question. The second is the per-item verification budget. National dates are well covered by the press, but Naples-specific figures are thin: the category-22 TARI tariff, the dehors coefficient in Allegato B, whether CUP avvisi go to IO, the SIAE tariff for a small pizzeria. Several of those remain open.

## Conclusion

This research changes the problem. A "personal obligations feed" sounds like an integration project: connect to AdE, INPS, the Comune. It turns out to be a reconciliation project. Public rules plus a good profile produce the expected ledger. The owner's own inboxes and studios supply most of the evidence. One monthly, owner-attended look at the cassetto fiscale confirms the bulk of the money, because F24 funnels nearly every payment into a single record. The parts that can't be automated (SPID, IO, SEND documents, EquiPro, PDND) are exactly the parts the design doesn't need. The genuinely hard and ongoing cost is editorial: keeping dates, proroghe and Naples tariffs current with sources each year. That is the work pratiche-napoli's claim-level verification already does.

The feed's distinctive value is not reminding Gennaro of the 16th, which his studios do. It is catching what falls between silos. The TARI acconto he owes whether or not the postman comes. The CUP rata whose date the Comune moved. The POS link nobody tells him about. The avviso clock that started in a PEC he never opened. The F24 the studio thought it sent. The shape to watch for is the AdE interoperability catalogue. If it adds an F24 Versamenti flow that a taxpayer can activate for himself, the attended read becomes an API call, and "whether it's done" becomes as automatic as "what you owe".
